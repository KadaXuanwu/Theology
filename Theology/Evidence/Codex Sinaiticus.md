points:
  - Oldest complete New Testament, mid 4th century. Most of it in the British Library, parts in Leipzig, St Catherine's Monastery and St Petersburg.
  - Also carries much of the Greek Old Testament, so it doubles as a Septuagint witness.
  - It includes the Epistle of Barnabas and the Shepherd of Hermas, which shows the canon was not settled in the form we have now.
