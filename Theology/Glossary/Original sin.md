# Description
The Christian doctrine that Adam's first sin left every later human in "an inherited state of disordered desire and ignorance".[^timpe-sep] Versions divide on whether that state also makes each person guilty. Augustine's includes an inherited "common guilt", while the denial of original guilt "is found in the Catholic tradition ... and many of the Orthodox traditions".[^timpe-sep] The term does not mean the first sin itself, nor a person's own sins.[^timpe-sep]
