# Description
The archaeological period of the southern Levant between the [[Middle Bronze Age]] and the [[Iron Age]], roughly 1550 to 1200 BCE.[^frevel-2023] It is divided into Late Bronze I and Late Bronze II, and the same work's table of periods runs its last phase to about 1150 BCE.[^frevel-2023-table]
