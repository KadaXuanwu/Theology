# Description
The account on which the solar system formed from a cloud of gas and dust around the young Sun, with the planets forming in the flat disk it became. Wilhelm Kley writes that "the flat structure of the Solar System indicates that this process has taken place within a protoplanetary disk, the Solar Nebula", and that this hypothesis "was already the basis of the formation theories of Kant and Laplace".[^kley-2017] The Observatoire de Paris dictionary dates Kant's version to 1755 and Laplace's to 1796.[^heydari-malayeri]
