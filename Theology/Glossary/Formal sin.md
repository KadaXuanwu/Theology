# Description
A sin committed knowingly and freely, and so imputable to the person, as against a material sin, an act contrary to God's law that the person does not know to be wrong or does not freely choose. Arthur Charles O'Neil's *Catholic Encyclopedia* entry says "formal sin is committed when the agent freely transgresses the law as shown him by his conscience", while an act contrary to the law "but is not known to be such by the agent constitutes a material sin".[^oneil-1912] He adds that "an involuntary transgression of the law even in a grave matter is not a formal but a material sin".[^oneil-1912]
