# Description
In decision theory, the probability-weighted average of the values an option could produce. R. A. Briggs states it for utilities: an act's expected utility is "a weighted average of the utilities of each of its possible outcomes".[^briggs-sep] Each outcome is weighted by the probability that the act leads to it, and the theory's rule is to choose the option with the highest such average.[^briggs-sep] Over many repeated trials of the same gamble, the average gain per trial is highly likely to come close to the gamble's expected value.[^briggs-sep]
