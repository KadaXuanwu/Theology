# Description
What a wrongdoer renders to the one wronged to pay the moral debt the wrong created, and in the doctrine of the atonement what is rendered to God for sin. One definition the *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* gives is that "to offer satisfaction for wrong W is to fulfill one's moral debt of wrongdoing for W".[^thurow-sep] It is not the narrower sacramental sense, where satisfaction is the step of the Catholic sacrament of penance that follows contrition and confession.[^thurow-sep]
