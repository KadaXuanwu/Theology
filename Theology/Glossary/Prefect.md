# Description
A Roman official appointed by a magistrate, and under the empire by the emperor, for a fixed period and a special task.[^lendering-2020-prefect] Jona Lendering lists Pontius Pilate, "the praefectus Judaea between 26 and 36", among the prefects of equestrian rank.[^lendering-2020-prefect] The title differs from [[Procurator|procurator]], the word Tacitus uses for Pilate.[^lendering-2020-procurator]
