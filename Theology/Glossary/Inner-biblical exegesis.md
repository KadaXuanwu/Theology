# Description
Exegesis and interpretation found within the Bible itself.[^garber-2007] Michael Fishbane's *Biblical Interpretation in Ancient Israel* sorts these forms as scribal, legal, theological and prophetic.[^garber-2007] Yair Zakovitch, writing of what he calls inner-biblical interpretation, describes it as "the light that one biblical text casts onto another", used "to solve a problem within the interpreted text or to adapt the interpreted text to the beliefs and ideas of the interpreter".[^zakovitch-2010] In his account the interpreting text may stand far from the one it interprets, sit next to it or be built into it.[^zakovitch-2010]
