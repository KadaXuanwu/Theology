# Description
Under the Roman empire, the governor of a senatorial province.[^lendering-2020-proconsul] Jona Lendering notes that these governors "were usually called proconsuls, although they were in fact former praetors", and that they typically served twelve months.[^lendering-2020-proconsul] In the Republic the word meant a former consul whose term in office was prolonged.[^lendering-2020-proconsul]
