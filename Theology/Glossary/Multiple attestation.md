# Description
A criterion of authenticity in historical Jesus research: a story or saying counts as better attested when it is found in more than one independent source. Rafael Rodríguez glosses it as "the presence of a story in more than one independent source, such as Q and Mark".[^rodriguez-2017] Q is the lost source that, on the Two-Source Theory, Matthew and Luke used beside Mark.[^rodriguez-2017]
