# Description
Imperfect contrition: sorrow for sin that arises from a motive short of the love of God, such as fear of hell, as against perfect contrition, which arises from love of God. The *Catechism of the Catholic Church* says it "is born of the consideration of sin's ugliness or the fear of eternal damnation and the other penalties threatening the sinner".[^catechism] By itself it cannot obtain forgiveness of grave sins, though it disposes a person to obtain it in the sacrament of Penance.[^catechism][^hanna-1907] It is not the ordinary sense of wearing down, though the word comes from the Latin *attero*, "to wear away by rubbing".[^hanna-1907]
