# Description
The Samaritan community's version of the five books of Moses, which remains its sacred text. Terry Giles calls it "one textual tradition (in scholarly terms, a 'recension') that descends from an ancient and influential form of the five Books of Moses", beside an early form of the [[Masoretic Text]], the Septuagint and others.[^giles-2017] It is not the same as the "Pre-Samaritan" texts from the Judean Desert, which share many of its editorial features but not the ones that favour the Samaritan sect.[^giles-2017] Giles takes it to be a sectarian edit of that text type, probably made between the first century BCE and the first century CE.[^giles-2017]
