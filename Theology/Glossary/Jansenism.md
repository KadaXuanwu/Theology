# Description
A seventeenth century Catholic theology of grace, inspired by the Dutch theologian Cornelius Jansen and his posthumously published *Augustinus* (1640).[^clarke-wood-sep] Desmond Clarke and William Wood call it "a starkly exclusive interpretation of how God's assistance enabled Fallen human beings to recover from the effect of Original Sin by the influence of 'efficacious grace'".[^clarke-wood-sep] In May 1653 Pope Innocent X condemned five propositions about grace said to be found in Jansen's book.[^clarke-wood-sep] Pascal's *Provincial Letters* defended the Jansenist theologian Antoine Arnauld against the censure that followed.[^clarke-wood-sep]
