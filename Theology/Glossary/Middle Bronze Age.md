# Description
The archaeological period of the southern Levant between the Early Bronze Age and the [[Late Bronze Age]], roughly 2000 to 1550 BCE.[^frevel-2023] Those figures are conventional: one study calls them "merely dates of convenience", with "little consensus" on when the period began or ended.[^cohen-2017]
