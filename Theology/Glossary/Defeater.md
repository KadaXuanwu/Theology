# Description
A reason or further belief that takes away the support a belief had. Robert Koons reports John Pollock's two kinds. Rebutting defeaters "give one a prima facie reason for believing the denial of the original conclusion". Undercutting defeaters "give one a reason for doubting that the usual relationship between the premises and the conclusion hold in the given case".[^koons-sep] On Alvin Plantinga's account a [[Properly basic belief|properly basic]] belief in God is open to defeat.[^bolos-scott-iep] Anthony Bolos and Kyle Scott report that it can be kept if a "defeater-defeater" answers the defeater.[^bolos-scott-iep]
