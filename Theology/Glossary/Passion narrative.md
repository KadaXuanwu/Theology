# Description
A Gospel's account of the Passion of Jesus: the betrayal, the Last Supper, the arrest, the appearances before Caiaphas and Pilate, the crucifixion and the burial.[^barnes-1911] In Mark, Nathanael Vette takes it as chapters 14–15.[^vette-2020]
