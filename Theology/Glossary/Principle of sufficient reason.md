# Description
The principle that everything must have a reason, cause or ground, or in a simple formulation for facts, that "for every fact F, there must be a sufficient reason why F is the case".[^melamed-lin-2023] Yitzhak Melamed and Martin Lin call it "a family of principles", formed by restricting a general schema, that for every x there is a sufficient reason for x, and by giving it different degrees of modal strength.[^melamed-lin-2023]
