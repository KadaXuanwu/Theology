# Description
A collective name for a mixture of groups, not one people, whose home Egyptian texts give as "the islands in the sea" or "the middle of the sea".[^frevel-2023] Egyptian sources first record fighting them under Merneptah, late in the 13th century BCE, and Ramesses III fought them in the Nile Delta about 1175 BCE.[^frevel-2023]
