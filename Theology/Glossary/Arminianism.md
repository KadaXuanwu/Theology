# Description
The theology that follows the Dutch theologian Jacobus Arminius in holding that God offers saving grace to all and that people can freely resist it.[^loughlin-1907] Thomas Talbott's *Stanford Encyclopedia* entry says "the Arminians hold that God offers his grace to all human beings, though many are those who freely reject it and eventually seal their fate in hell forever".[^talbott-sep] The same entry says they "accept the so-called [[Libertarian free will|libertarian]] understanding of free will".[^talbott-sep] Applied to hell, an Arminian view holds that those in hell are there by their own choice to reject that grace.[^talbott-sep]
