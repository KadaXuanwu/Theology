# Description
Fear of punishment that makes a person turn to God in order to escape the punishment, as against filial fear, which fears offending God. John Hardon's *Modern Catholic Dictionary* defines it as "the fear of punishment for wrongdoing, without being motivated by honor or a sense of duty, and least of all by love".[^hardon-1980] Aquinas draws the line the same way: "if a man turn to God and adhere to Him, through fear of punishment, it will be servile fear; but if it be on account of fear of committing a fault, it will be filial fear".[^aquinas-st-ii-ii-19]
