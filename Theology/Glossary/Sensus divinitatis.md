# Description
Latin for "sense of divinity": in Alvin Plantinga's epistemology, a faculty that produces beliefs about God directly, without inference from an argument. Anthony Bolos and Kyle Scott give his statement that such a faculty "in a wide variety of circumstances produces in us beliefs about God".[^bolos-scott-iep] Plantinga takes the term from John Calvin, who held, in the entry's words, that God "created humans with an inner awareness of himself".[^bolos-scott-iep] Plantinga's use is a faculty inside a model of [[Warrant|warrant]]. The entry notes that "it is not Plantinga's intention to show that this faculty exists or that this really is the way that religious beliefs come about".[^bolos-scott-iep]
