# Description
The hypothetical, reconstructed text of a work as it stood before its copying began, the form of text from which the transmission started.[^wachtel-2011] Klaus Wachtel and Michael Holmes quote Gerd Mink's definition at p. 6: "The initial text is a hypothetical, reconstructed text, as it presumably existed, according to the hypothesis, before the beginning of its copying."[^wachtel-2011] It need not be the author's own text: the Institut für Neutestamentliche Textforschung in Münster says the initial text "is not necessarily identical to the text of the author".[^intf-initial]
