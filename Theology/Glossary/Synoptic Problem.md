# Description
The question of how the Gospels of Matthew, Mark and Luke are related as written texts, since they often give the same words and stories in the same order. Sarah E. Rollens: "The question of the literary relationship among these gospels is also known as the Synoptic Problem."[^rollens-2022] Most scholars, she writes, agree that Mark was a source for Matthew and Luke.[^rollens-2022] Among those who do, the Two-Source Hypothesis adds a second source, Q, made up mostly of sayings, and the Farrer Hypothesis has Luke use Matthew as well.[^rollens-2022] The Griesbach Hypothesis instead has Mark combine Matthew and Luke.[^rollens-2022] "Synoptic" on its own names the three Gospels, which leaves out John.[^rollens-2022]
