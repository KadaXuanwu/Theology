# Description
A Roman official appointed by a magistrate or the emperor, at first to manage money and estates.[^lendering-2020-procurator] Under Claudius the first procurators appear "with the full powers of a provincial governor", and from the reign of Tiberius on they were drawn from the equestrian order.[^lendering-2020-procurator] Tacitus calls Pontius Pilate a procurator, though an inscription shows that he was a [[Prefect|prefect]].[^lendering-2020-procurator]
