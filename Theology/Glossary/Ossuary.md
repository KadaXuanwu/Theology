# Description
A small chest "in which the bones of the dead were placed after the flesh had decayed".[^avi-yonah-gibson-2007] Michael Avi-Yonah and Shimon Gibson describe small stone chests used for this second burial among Jews "between c. 40 b.c.e. and 135 c.e.", known mainly from tombs around Jerusalem.[^avi-yonah-gibson-2007]
