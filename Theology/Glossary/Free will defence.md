# Description
Alvin Plantinga's reply to the logical problem of evil: God may not have been able to create creatures capable of moral good without creating creatures capable of moral evil, so God and evil can both exist.[^beebe-iep] It is a defence and not a theodicy, an attempt to undermine the argument "without offering a positive account of why God allows evil and suffering".[^beebe-iep] A free will theodicy goes further and claims that "libertarian free will is of great value" and is God's reason.[^tooley-sep] The defence assumes [[Libertarian free will|libertarian free will]].[^beebe-iep]
