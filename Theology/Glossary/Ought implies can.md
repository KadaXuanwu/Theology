# Description
The principle that a person can be morally required to do something only if it is within their power to do it. Terrance McConnell states it as "if an agent is morally required to do an action, it must be within the agent's power to do it".[^mcconnell-2022] Applied to belief, it means that if people cannot control what they believe, they cannot be obliged to believe anything. Richard Feldman denies the principle for belief, holding that duties of belief are "role oughts" that bind us as believers even without direct control.[^boespflug-2024]
