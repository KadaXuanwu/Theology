# Description
The kingdom over all Israel that the Bible presents before the division into Israel in the north and Judah in the south, in Christian Frevel's words the "united monarchy under [Saul,] David, and Solomon".[^frevel-2023] Frevel reports that research is divided over whether such a kingdom existed, under the heading "Great Empire, United Monarchy, or Chiefdom?".[^frevel-2023-monarchy]
