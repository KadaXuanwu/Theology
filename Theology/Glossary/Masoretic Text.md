# Description
The standard Hebrew text of the Hebrew Bible: a medieval text whose consonants go back to the Second Temple period, with vowel signs, accents and the Masoretes' notes added in the early Middle Ages. Emanuel Tov calls it "a medieval text with roots in antiquity", and dates the full text, with all its elements, from the ninth century on.[^tov-2017] Its consonantal base as attested in the Second Temple period is usually called the proto-Masoretic text, "although sometimes also, anachronistically, as the Masoretic Text".[^tov-2017]
