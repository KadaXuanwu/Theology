# Description
God's knowledge, prior to any decision to create, of what any possible free creature would freely do in any circumstances it might be placed in. The doctrine is Luis de Molina's, and Hugh McCann and Daniel Johnson write that God knows these truths "prior to any creative act on his part".[^mccann-2022] The truths it covers have the form "If person S were in circumstances C, S would freely do X"; they are meant to be contingent, they come before God's creative will, and he uses them in deciding what to create.[^hunt-2026] It is named for standing between God's natural knowledge and his free knowledge, and *scientia media* is its Latin name.[^laing-iep] It is meant to fit [[Libertarian free will|libertarian]] freedom to providence.[^laing-iep]
