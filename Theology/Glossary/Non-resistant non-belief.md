# Description
Not believing that God exists without having shut the door on a relationship with God oneself. The term is J. L. Schellenberg's and the resistance it rules out runs wider than a conscious refusal: he counts "careless investigation of one sort or another in relation to the existence of God", consorting only with people who carelessly fail to believe, and "just over time mentally drifting, with her own acquiescence, away from any place where she could convincingly be met by evidence of God".[^sep-hiddenness] Reading it as only ruling out stubborn refusal is the common mistake. Schellenberg's 1993 book called the same category reasonable or inculpable non-belief, and he later called that focus on culpability a mistake.[^sep-hiddenness]
