# Description
A person's tendency to become deeply engrossed in what they perceive or imagine. T. M. Luhrmann and colleagues define it as "an individual's personal tendency to be engrossed in sensory or imagined events".[^luhrmann-2021] They note that it "is commonly considered to be a personality trait, although it may be sensitive to experience or training".[^luhrmann-2021] A 2019 review argues that the trait "captures a general proclivity for having spiritual experiences".[^lifshitz-2019] In this sense it is a trait measured on a psychological scale, not the everyday word for being absorbed in a task.[^luhrmann-2021]
