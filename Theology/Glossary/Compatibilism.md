# Description
The thesis that free will is compatible with determinism.[^mckenna-coates-2024] Since free will is usually taken to be needed for moral responsibility, it is also put as the thesis that responsibility is compatible with determinism.[^mckenna-coates-2024] Its denial is [[Incompatibilism]].
