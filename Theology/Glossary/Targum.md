# Description
An Aramaic translation of a book of the Hebrew Bible, often paraphrasing the Hebrew and used as an interpretation of it. Wilhelm Bacher defines the word as "The Aramaic translation of the Bible" and speaks of "the paraphrastic style of translation affected by the Targumim generally".[^bacher-1905] The word also names single works, such as Targum Onkelos on the Pentateuch and Targum Jonathan on the Prophets.[^bacher-1905] In mishnaic usage the noun could mean a translation of the Bible into any language.[^bacher-1905]
