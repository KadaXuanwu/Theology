# Description
A quotation of scripture in Matthew's Gospel that the narrator adds as a comment on the story, introduced by a formula built on the verb "to fulfil".[^kirk-2008][^menken-2002] Maarten Menken calls the introduction "Matthew's standard but slightly flexible formula".[^menken-2002] He describes such a quotation as "a reflection after the event, that can be omitted without a loss of flow to the story line".[^menken-2002] The same quotations are also called formula quotations or formula citations, in German Reflexionszitate or Erfüllungszitate.[^kirk-2008][^august-2018]

Jared August counts fourteen places where Matthew uses the verb with a passage or theme of scripture: [Matthew 1:22; 2:15, 17, 23; 4:14; 5:17; 8:17; 12:17; 13:14, 35; 21:4; 26:54, 56; 27:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%201:22;2:15,17,23;4:14;5:17;8:17;12:17;13:14,35;21:4;26:54,56;27:9&version=NIV).[^august-2018] In his analysis ten of them cite a specific passage (1:22; 2:15, 17; 4:14; 8:17; 12:17; 13:14, 35; 21:4; 27:9) and four develop a general theme (2:23; 5:17; 26:54, 56).[^august-2018]
