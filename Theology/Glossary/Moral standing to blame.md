# Description
Whether the person who would blame is entitled to blame, a fact about the blamer and a separate question from whether the wrongdoer is blameworthy.[^tognazzini-coates-2024] Hypocritical blame is the paradigm case of a blamer without it, and complicity is one of several other ways it is said to be lost.[^tognazzini-coates-2024] Complicity is the charge that the blamer is "objectionably involved in the very act that they are, at this very moment, condemning".[^tognazzini-coates-2024]
