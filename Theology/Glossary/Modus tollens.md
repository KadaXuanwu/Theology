# Description
A form of inference that concludes the denial of a conditional's antecedent from the conditional and the denial of its consequent: if P then Q, not Q, so not P. Kevin Klement's entry describes it as "deducing the negation of the antecedent of a conditional from the conditional and the negation of its consequent", and gives the name as Latin for "denying mode".[^klement-iep] Bradley Dowden's entry calls it "a valid form of argument using the conditional" and says the fallacy of denying the antecedent is often mistaken for it.[^dowden-iep]
