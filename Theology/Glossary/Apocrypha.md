# Description
The books, or parts of books, found in the Septuagint, the Greek translation of the Jewish scriptures, but not in the Jewish [[Canon|canon]] of the Hebrew scriptures.[^powell-2009] Greek Orthodox and Roman Catholic Christians "regard them as scripture and often prefer to call them deuterocanonical books", while "Most Protestants treat them with respect but do not grant them the status of scripture".[^powell-2009] Greek Orthodox churches also accept 3 and 4 Maccabees and Psalm 151.[^powell-2009] The word comes from the Greek for "hidden" and is also used for early Christian writings outside the New Testament, such as the apocryphal acts of the apostles.[^denzey-lewis-2022] The vault means the Old Testament books.
