# Description
Michel de Montaigne was a French essayist who sat as a magistrate in the Parlement of Bordeaux until he sold his office in 1570, and was later mayor of Bordeaux.[^iep-montaigne][^catholic-encyclopedia-montaigne] In the *Essais* he writes that "We are Christians by the same title that we are Perigordians or Germans" (II.12).[^montaigne-essays]

# Work
- *Essais*, books I and II (1580).[^iep-montaigne]
- *Essais*, with book III added (1588).[^iep-montaigne][^catholic-encyclopedia-montaigne]
- *Essais*, posthumous edition by Marie de Gournay (1595).[^iep-montaigne]
- *Journal de voyage*, on his journey of 1580–1581, first published in the 18th century.[^iep-montaigne]

# Stated Position
He submits the *Essais* to the censure of the Catholic Church, "into which I was born and in which I will die" (I.56).[^montaigne-essays]
