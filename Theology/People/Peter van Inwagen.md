# Description
Peter van Inwagen is a philosopher at Notre Dame known for work on free will, material objects and the problem of evil.[^nd-vaninwagen] His contribution to the hiddenness debate asks what a public divine sign could actually establish.[^vaninwagen-2002]

# Work
- *An Essay on Free Will* (1983).[^nd-vaninwagen]
- *Material Beings* (1990).[^nd-vaninwagen]
- "What Is the Problem of the Hiddenness of God?", in Daniel Howard-Snyder and Paul K. Moser (eds), *Divine Hiddenness: New Essays* (2002), 24–32.[^vaninwagen-2002]
- *The Problem of Evil* (2006), his Gifford Lectures.[^nd-vaninwagen]

# Stated Position
He writes in an autobiographical essay that since his baptism he has been an Episcopalian and regards himself as a Catholic.[^vaninwagen-1994] In the same piece he dates his first church attendance to September 1982 and his baptism to the following May.
