# Description
Michael Bergmann is a professor of philosophy at Purdue University, with a PhD from the University of Notre Dame.[^bergmann-cv] He works on epistemology and the philosophy of religion, and has been president of the Society of Christian Philosophers.[^purdue-bergmann]

# Work
- *Justification without Awareness: A Defense of Epistemic Externalism* (Oxford University Press, 2006).[^bergmann-cv]
- "Religious Disagreement and Rational Demotion", in *Oxford Studies in Philosophy of Religion* 6, ed. Jonathan Kvanvig (Oxford University Press, 2015), 21–57.[^bergmann-cv]
- *Radical Skepticism and Epistemic Intuition* (Oxford University Press, 2021).[^bergmann-cv]
- "Reformed Epistemology", in *The Cambridge Handbook of Religious Epistemology*, ed. Jonathan Fuqua, John Greco and Tyler McNabb (Cambridge University Press, 2023), 41–55.[^bergmann-2023]
