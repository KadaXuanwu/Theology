# Description
John Owen was an English Puritan theologian and Independent minister, Dean of Christ Church, Oxford, and Vice Chancellor of the university.[^fasti-anglicanae][^dnb-owen] He wrote on the atonement, sin and communion with God, and argued that God's justice requires sin to be punished.[^dnb-owen][^owen-1653]

# Work
- *Salus Electorum, Sanguis Jesu, or The Death of Death in the Death of Christ* (1648).[^dnb-owen]
- *Diatriba de Divina Justitia* (Oxford, 1653), in English *A Dissertation on Divine Justice*.[^owen-1653]
- *Of the Mortification of Sin in Believers* (1656).[^dnb-owen]
- *Of Communion with God the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost* (1657).[^dnb-owen]

# Stated Position
In the *Dissertation on Divine Justice* he says he had once held the view he is rejecting there, that God may punish sin or not as he wills: "to the praise and glory of that truth, of which I am now a servant, I freely confess to have been my case" (I.2).[^owen-1653]
