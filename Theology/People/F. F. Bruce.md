# Description
F. F. Bruce was a biblical scholar and John Rylands Professor of Biblical Criticism and Exegesis at the University of Manchester.[^ct-bruce][^gnd-bruce] He taught Greek at Edinburgh and Leeds before that.[^ct-bruce]

# Work
- *The New Testament Documents: Are They Reliable?*, first published in 1943. Its fourth edition is catalogued as *Are the New Testament Documents Reliable?*, and the fifth, revised edition under the present title, with the note that the previous edition had the earlier one.[^bruce-preface][^hbz-bruce-fourth][^loc-bruce-fifth][^hbz-bruce-fifth] The preface to the fifth edition is dated April 1959.[^bruce-preface]
- *The Acts of the Apostles: The Greek Text with Introduction and Commentary* (Tyndale Press, 1951).[^ct-bruce][^k10-bruce-acts]

# Stated Position
In the preface to the fifth edition of *The New Testament Documents* he writes that "history and theology are inextricably intertwined in the gospel of our salvation, which owes its eternal and universal validity to certain events which happened in Palestine when Tiberius ruled the Roman Empire".[^bruce-preface]
