# Description
Travis Dumsday is a philosopher at Concordia University of Edmonton who works on divine hiddenness and on the metaphysics of science.[^concordia-dumsday] He has published a long run of papers on hiddenness, each proposing a different reason God might stay hidden, and his survey of the free will reply is the one the vault uses for where that reply stands.[^dumsday-2010]

# Work
- "Divine Hiddenness, Free-Will, and the Victims of Wrongdoing", *Faith and Philosophy* 27/4 (2010), 423–438.[^dumsday-2010]
- "Divine hiddenness and special revelation", *Religious Studies* 51/2 (2015), 241–259.[^dumsday-cv]
- "C. S. Lewis on the Problem of Divine Hiddenness", *Anglican Theological Review* 97/1 (2015), 33–51.[^dumsday-2015]
- *Dispositionalism and the Metaphysics of Science* (2019).[^dumsday-cv]

# Stated Position
He says in an interview that he is Orthodox and tries to bring his faith to bear on some of his philosophical work, and that "only in the Orthodox Church can we confidently partake of the genuine body and blood of Christ in the eucharist".[^dumsday-interview]
