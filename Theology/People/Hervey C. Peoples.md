# Description
Hervey C. Peoples is a molecular geneticist and evolutionary anthropologist who worked at Cambridge with Frank W. Marlowe. She is lead author of the survey of religion across forager societies that the vault's material on animism rests on.[^peoples-site][^peoples-2016]

# Work
- *The Human Question: What People Believe About Evolution, Human Origins, and the Beginning of Life* (2003).[^peoples-site]
- "Subsistence and the Evolution of Religion", with Frank W. Marlowe, *Human Nature* 23 (2012).[^peoples-site]
- "Hunter-Gatherers and the Origins of Religion", with Pavel Duda and Frank W. Marlowe, *Human Nature* 27 (2016), 261–282.[^peoples-2016]
