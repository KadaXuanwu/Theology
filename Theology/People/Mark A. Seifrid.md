# Description
Mark A. Seifrid is a New Testament scholar and emeritus professor of exegetical theology at Concordia Seminary in St. Louis, after 23 years teaching New Testament at the Southern Baptist Theological Seminary. He works on Paul's teaching on justification and on Romans.[^concordia-2015][^concordia-emeritus][^seifrid-1998]

# Work
- *Justification by Faith: The Origin and Development of a Central Pauline Theme* (Brill, 1992).[^brill-seifrid]
- "Natural Revelation and the Purpose of the Law in Romans", *Tyndale Bulletin* 49/1 (1998), 115–129.[^seifrid-1998]
- *The Second Letter to the Corinthians*, Pillar New Testament Commentary (Eerdmans, 2014).[^eerdmans-seifrid]

# Stated Position
On joining Concordia Seminary in 2015 he said: "The spirit of God, along with my continuing interaction with Scripture, really convinced me of the truth embodied in the Lutheran Confessions." In the same release he said: "I know Jesus is mine, and I am His."[^concordia-2015]
