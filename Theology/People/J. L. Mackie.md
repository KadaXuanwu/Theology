# Description
J. L. Mackie was a philosopher who held chairs at Otago, Sydney and York before he became a fellow of University College, Oxford.[^campbell-mackie] He wrote on causation, on ethics and on the arguments for and against the existence of God.[^campbell-mackie]

# Work
- "Evil and Omnipotence" (1955).[^mackie-1955]
- *The Cement of the Universe* (1974).[^campbell-mackie]
- *Ethics: Inventing Right and Wrong* (1977).[^campbell-mackie]
- *The Miracle of Theism* (1982), published after his death.[^campbell-mackie]

# Stated Position
In "Evil and Omnipotence" he holds that the problem of evil shows religious beliefs to be not merely unsupported by reason but "positively irrational", because parts of the essential theological doctrine contradict one another.[^mackie-1955]
