# Description
Michael J. Kruger is a New Testament scholar and Samuel C. Patterson Chancellor's Professor of New Testament and Early Christianity at Reformed Theological Seminary, after twelve years as president of its Charlotte campus.[^rts-2025] He took his PhD at the University of Edinburgh, works on the origins of the New Testament [[Canon|canon]] and has served as president of the Evangelical Theological Society.[^kruger-about][^rts-2025]

# Work
- *Canon Revisited* (Crossway, 2012).[^kruger-about]
- "The Definition of the Term 'Canon': Exclusive or Multi-Dimensional?", *Tyndale Bulletin* 63/1 (2012), 1–20.[^kruger-2012]
- *The Question of Canon* (IVP Academic, 2013).[^kruger-about]

# Stated Position
In an interview with *Credo Magazine*, excerpted on his blog in 2015, he said: "In addition to the date of the canon's reception, there is also the question of the date of the canon's existence. And this latter issue can only be discussed when theological considerations are allowed into the discussion (e.g., canonical books are given by the inspiration of the Spirit)."[^kruger-2015]
