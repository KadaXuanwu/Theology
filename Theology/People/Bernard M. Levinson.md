# Description
Bernard M. Levinson is a scholar of the Hebrew Bible who holds the Berman Family Chair of Jewish Studies and Hebrew Bible at the University of Minnesota.[^umn-levinson] His work is on biblical and Near Eastern law and on the Bible's relation to later Western intellectual history.[^wiko-levinson]

# Work
- *Deuteronomy and the Hermeneutics of Legal Innovation* (1997).[^wiko-levinson]
- "You Must Not Add Anything to What I Command You: Paradoxes of Canon and Authorship in Ancient Israel", *Numen* 50/1 (2003), 1–51.[^levinson-2003]
- *Legal Revision and Religious Renewal in Ancient Israel* (2008).[^wiko-levinson]
