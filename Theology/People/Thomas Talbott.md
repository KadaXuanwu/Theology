# Description
Thomas Talbott is a philosopher, emeritus at Willamette University, where he taught for thirty four years. *The Inescapable Love of God* is the analytic case for Christian universalism, that no one is finally lost.[^willamette-talbott][^loc-talbott]

# Work
- *The Inescapable Love of God* (1999).[^talbott-site]
- "Universalism", in Jerry Walls (ed.), *The Oxford Handbook of Eschatology* (2008).[^talbott-site]
- *Understanding the Free-Will Controversy* (2022).[^talbott-site]

# Stated Position
He writes as a Christian universalist, describing his aim as calling attention to a minority Christian tradition of God's all inclusive love, and saying that much of what passes for orthodox theology is riddled with logical impossibilities.[^talbott-about]
