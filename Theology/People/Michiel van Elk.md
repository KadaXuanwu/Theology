# Description
Michiel van Elk is a cognitive psychologist at Leiden University who works on the psychology and neuroscience of religious and psychedelic experience. He founded its PRSM lab.[^leiden-van-elk][^loc-van-elk]

# Work
- *De gelovige geest: op zoek naar de biologische en psychologische wortels van religie* (2012).[^loc-van-elk]
- "Brain mechanisms in religion and spirituality: An integrative predictive processing framework", with André Aleman, *Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews* (2017).[^leiden-van-elk]
- "Seeking the supernatural: the Interactive Religious Experience Model", with Neil Van Leeuwen, *Religion, Brain and Behavior* (2018).[^leiden-van-elk]

# Stated Position
He writes that he was raised in a Pentecostal and evangelical family and broke with that faith in January 2005.[^van-elk-psychologist]
