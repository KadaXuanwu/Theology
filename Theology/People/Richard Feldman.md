# Description
Richard Feldman is University Professor of Philosophy at the University of Rochester, working on epistemology and the ethics of belief.[^rochester-feldman] With Earl Conee he is one of the two names on evidentialism, and the vault cites him for the account on which the duty to believe is a duty to get the belief right rather than to try hard.[^feldman-2000]

# Work
- *Reason and Argument* (Prentice-Hall, 1993; 2nd edn 1999).[^feldman-cv]
- "The Ethics of Belief", *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 60/3 (2000), 667–695.[^feldman-2000]
- *Epistemology* (Prentice Hall, 2003).[^feldman-cv]
- *Evidentialism*, with Earl Conee (Oxford University Press, 2004).[^feldman-cv]
