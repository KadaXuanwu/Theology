# Description
Martin Dibelius was a German Protestant theologian and Heidelberg professor who studied the prehistory of the New Testament's literary texts, above all the first three Gospels and Acts.[^ndb-dibelius][^jansen-1996] His *Formgeschichte des Evangeliums* gave its name to a line of research that [[Rudolf Bultmann]] also pursued.[^ndb-dibelius]

# Work
- *Die Formgeschichte des Evangeliums* (Mohr, 1919; 2nd edn 1933).[^ndb-dibelius][^hbz-1919][^hbz-1933]
- "La signification religieuse des récits évangéliques de la Passion" (1933), a lecture given at the Faculté libre de théologie protestante de Paris on 26 November 1932.[^dibelius-1933]
- *Jesus* (de Gruyter, 1939).[^ndb-dibelius][^hbz-jesus]
- *Aufsätze zur Apostelgeschichte*, edited by Heinrich Greeven (Evangelische Verlagsanstalt, 1951).[^ndb-dibelius][^hbz-aufsaetze]

# Stated Position
No published statement of his own position on the truth of Christianity was found.
