# Description
Robert Koch was a German bacteriologist who identified the organisms behind anthrax, tuberculosis and cholera, and set out the postulates for showing that a microbe causes a disease. He took the 1905 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for the tuberculosis work.[^nobel-koch][^rki-koch]

# Work
- *Die Ätiologie der Milzbrand-Krankheit* (1876).[^rki-koch]
- *Untersuchungen über die Aetiologie der Wundinfectionskrankheiten* (1878).[^rki-koch]
- *Die Ätiologie der Tuberkulose* (1882).[^rki-koch]

# Stated Position
None found in print. His biographer Thomas Brock writes that religion never entered his life, which is a biographer's judgement rather than his own words.
