# Description
Randal Rauser is a theologian who taught historical theology at Taylor Seminary in Edmonton for twenty years, until 2023.[^rauser-2023] He has written an article and a book on the Old Testament commands to destroy the Canaanites.[^rauser-2009][^bedard-2022]

# Work
- "'Let Nothing that Breathes Remain Alive': On the Problem of Divinely Commanded Genocide", *Philosophia Christi* 11/1 (2009), 27–41.[^rauser-2009]
- *Jesus Loves Canaanites: Biblical Genocide in the Light of Moral Intuition* (2 Cup Press, 2021).[^bedard-2022]

# Stated Position
In the abstract of his 2009 article: "In this essay I argue that God did not command the Canaanite genocide."[^eps-rauser]
