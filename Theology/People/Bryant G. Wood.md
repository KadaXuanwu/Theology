# Description
Bryant G. Wood is an archaeologist with a doctorate from the University of Toronto who has served as director of research at the Associates for Biblical Research.[^southern-wood][^lc-wood] He directed the excavation at Khirbet el-Maqatir, which he proposes as the Ai of Joshua 7–8.[^southern-wood][^wood-2012]

# Work
- *The Sociology of Pottery in Ancient Palestine* (1990).[^bar-2020]
- "Did the Israelites Conquer Jericho? A New Look at the Archaeological Evidence" (1990).[^bas-wood-1990]
- "The Rise and Fall of the 13th-Century Exodus-Conquest Theory" (2005).[^wood-2005]

# Stated Position
"Biblical data clearly place the exodus-conquest in the 15th century bc, and extra-biblical evidence strongly supports this dating."[^wood-2005] He also writes that the evidence from Jericho, Ai and Hazor "correlates well with the biblical date and descriptions of those destructions."[^wood-2005]
