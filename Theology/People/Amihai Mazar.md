# Description
Amihai Mazar is an archaeologist of ancient Israel at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, where he held the Eleazar Sukenik Chair in the Archaeology of Israel and headed the Institute of Archaeology.[^ej-mazar] He excavated at Tel Qasile, Tel Batash, Beth Shean and Tel Rehov.[^ej-mazar]

# Work
- *Archaeology of the Land of the Bible, 10,000–586 B.C.E.* (1990).[^ej-mazar]
- "The Patriarchs, Exodus, and Conquest Narratives in Light of Archaeology", in Israel Finkelstein and Amihai Mazar, *The Quest for the Historical Israel* (2007).[^finkelstein-mazar-2007]

# Stated Position
"It is thus now accepted by all that archaeology in fact contradicts the biblical account of the Israelite Conquest as a discreet historical event led by one leader."[^finkelstein-mazar-2007] He adds that archaeology "may shed some light" on how memories rooted in the second millennium reached the Pentateuch and Joshua, and reads the burning of Hazor as "a reflection of historical memories" of the city's end.[^finkelstein-mazar-2007]
