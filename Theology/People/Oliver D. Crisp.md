# Description
Oliver D. Crisp is a theologian at St Andrews, where he co-directs the Logos Institute, and was at Fuller Theological Seminary from 2011 to 2019. He works in analytic theology and in the Reformed tradition.[^standrews-crisp][^loc-crisp]

# Work
- *Divinity and Humanity: The Incarnation Reconsidered* (2007).[^standrews-crisp]
- *Analytic Theology: New Essays in the Philosophy of Theology*, edited with Michael Rea (2009).[^standrews-crisp]
- *Deviant Calvinism: Broadening Reformed Theology* (2014).[^standrews-crisp]
