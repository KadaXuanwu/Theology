# Description
Karl Budde was a German Protestant Old Testament scholar who worked in the historical-critical school of Abraham Kuenen and Julius Wellhausen.[^ndb-budde] He wrote commentaries on Judges, Samuel, Isaiah 40–66, Job, the Song of Songs, Lamentations and Ecclesiastes, and studies of the history of Israelite religion.[^ndb-budde]

# Work
- *Das Buch der Richter* (1897).[^lobid-richter]
- "The So-Called 'Ebed-Yahweh Songs,' and the Meaning of the Term 'Servant of Yahweh' in Isaiah, Chaps. 40–55" (1899).[^budde-1899]
- *Die sogenannten Ebed-Jahwe-Lieder und die Bedeutung des Knechtes Jahwes in Jes. 40–55* (1900).[^lobid-ebed]
- *Die Religion des Volkes Israel bis zur Verbannung* (1900), whose third edition appeared in 1912 as *Die altisraelitische Religion*.[^lobid-religion][^ndb-budde]
- *Die Bücher Samuel* (1902).[^lobid-samuel]

# Stated Position
No published statement of his own position on the truth of Christianity was found.
