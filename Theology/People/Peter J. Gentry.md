# Description
Peter J. Gentry is an Old Testament and Septuagint scholar who has taught at the Southern Baptist Theological Seminary as Donald L. Williams Professor of Old Testament Interpretation.[^sbts-gentry] He earlier taught for fifteen years at Toronto Baptist Seminary and Bible College, took his PhD at the University of Toronto and directs the Hexapla Institute.[^sbts-gentry][^crossway-gentry]

# Work
- "The Septuagint and the Text of the Old Testament", *Bulletin for Biblical Research* 16/2 (2006), 193–218.[^gentry-2006]
- *Ecclesiastes*, Septuaginta: Vetus Testamentum Graecum 11/2 (Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 2019), the Göttingen edition.[^gentry-2019]
