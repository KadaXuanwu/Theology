# Description
Bernhard Duhm was a German Protestant Old Testament scholar who taught at Göttingen and then as full professor at Basel.[^ndb-duhm] He worked on the prophetic and poetic books, and his Isaiah commentary set four [[Servant songs|servant poems]] apart from Isaiah 40–55.[^ndb-duhm][^duhm-1902]

# Work
- *Die Theologie der Propheten* (Marcus, 1875).[^lobid-theologie]
- *Das Buch Jesaia* (Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1892; 2nd edn 1902). It prints the servant poems as Isaiah 42:1–4, 49:1–6, 50:4–9 and 52:13–53:12 and argues that their servant is an individual, not Israel as a collective.[^lobid-jesaia][^duhm-1902]
- *Israels Propheten* (Mohr, 1916; 2nd edn 1922).[^ndb-duhm][^lobid-propheten]

# Stated Position
No published statement of his own position on the truth of Christianity was found.
