# Description
Kenneth Einar Himma is a philosopher and legal theorist, a continuing guest professor at the Faculty of Law of the University of Zagreb. He has published widely in philosophy of law, philosophy of religion and information ethics.[^himma-oup] The vault cites him for his evaluation of the continuing sin response to the proportionality problem of hell.[^himma-2003]

# Work
- "Eternally incorrigible: the continuing-sin response to the proportionality problem of hell", *Religious Studies* 39/1 (2003), 61–78.[^himma-2003]
- "Christian Faith Without Belief That God Exists: A Defense of Pojman's Conception of Faith", *Faith and Philosophy* 23/1 (2006), 65–79.[^himma-2006]
- *Morality and the Nature of Law* (Oxford University Press, 2019).[^himma-2019]
- *Coercion and the Nature of Law* (Oxford University Press, 2020).[^himma-oup]
