# Description
Kenneth Einar Himma is a philosopher and legal theorist at the University of Washington School of Law, publishing mainly in philosophy of law and information ethics.[^himma-oup] The vault cites him for the continuing sin response to the proportionality problem of hell, which is a secondary strand in his work.[^himma-2003]

# Work
- "Eternally incorrigible: the continuing-sin response to the proportionality problem of hell", *Religious Studies* 39/1 (2003), 61–78.[^himma-2003]
- "Christian faith without belief that God exists: a defense of Pojman's conception of faith", *Faith and Philosophy* (2006).[^himma-oup]
- *Morality and the Nature of Law* (Oxford University Press, 2019).[^himma-oup]
- *Coercion and the Nature of Law* (Oxford University Press, 2020).[^himma-oup]
