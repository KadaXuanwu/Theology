# Description
Samuel David Luzzatto, known as Shadal, was an Italian Jewish philologist, poet and Bible commentator who taught at the rabbinical college of Padua.[^je-luzzatto][^gnd-luzzatto] He wrote on Hebrew grammar, the [[Targum]] of Onkelos and the text of the Hebrew Bible, and published a commentary on Isaiah.[^je-luzzatto]

# Work
- *Oheb Ger*, a guide to the Targum of Onkelos (Vienna, 1830).[^je-luzzatto]
- *Il Giudaismo Illustrato* (Padua, 1848).[^je-luzzatto]
- *Sefer Yesha'yah*, the Book of Isaiah with an Italian translation and a Hebrew commentary (Padua, 1855–1867).[^je-luzzatto] On the servant of [Isaiah 52:13–53:12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah%2052:13-53:12&version=NIV) it says, in the English of S. R. Driver and Ad. Neubauer: "For my own part, I understand it of Israel in exile."[^driver-neubauer-1877]

# Stated Position
Of the Christian reading of Isaiah 53 he wrote, in the English of S. R. Driver and Ad. Neubauer, that Christians "explain it in their usual manner; but they have been already answered" by earlier commentators.[^driver-neubauer-1877]
