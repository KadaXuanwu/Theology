# Description
Pierre-Simon Laplace was a French mathematician and astronomer whose work on the stability of the solar system closed the gap Newton had filled with occasional divine repair. He also wrote the standard early treatment of probability.[^mactutor-laplace][^britannica-laplace]

# Work
- *Exposition du système du monde* (1796).[^britannica-laplace]
- *Traité de mécanique céleste*, five volumes (1798 to 1827).[^britannica-laplace]
- *Théorie analytique des probabilités* (1812).[^britannica-laplace]

# Stated Position
None found in print, and the famous line about having no need of that hypothesis is reported speech he never wrote. Hervé Faye argued that what Laplace rejected was Newton's occasional divine correction of the planets rather than God.[^faye-1884]
