# Description
David C. Parker is a New Testament textual critic and Emeritus Professor of Digital Philology at the University of Birmingham.[^birmingham-parker][^loc-parker] He was founding director of its Institute for Textual Scholarship and Electronic Editing and is executive editor of the Gospel of John for the International Greek New Testament Project.[^birmingham-retirement]

# Work
- *The Living Text of the Gospels* (Cambridge University Press, 1997).[^parker-1997]
- "Textual Criticism and Theology", *The Expository Times* 118/12 (2007), 583–589.[^parker-2007]
- "Is 'Living Text' Compatible with 'Initial Text'? Editing the Gospel of John" (2011).[^parker-2011]
- *Textual Scholarship and the Making of the New Testament* (Oxford University Press, 2012), the Lyell Lectures of 2011.[^birmingham-parker]
