# Description
Tyler Paytas is a philosopher at Australian Catholic University who works on Kant's ethics and on philosophy of religion.[^paytas-cv] He defends an autonomy based account of divine hiddenness drawn from Kant, which grants that a person could still transgress while certain of God and locates the loss elsewhere.[^paytas-2017]

# Work
- "God's Awful Majesty Before Our Eyes: Kant's Moral Justification for Divine Hiddenness", *Kantian Review* 22/1 (2017), 133–157.[^paytas-2017]
- "Of Providence and Puppet Shows: Divine Hiddenness as Kantian Theodicy", *Faith and Philosophy* 36/1 (2019), 56–80.[^paytas-2019]
- *Kantian and Sidgwickian Ethics: The Cosmos of Duty Above and the Moral Law Within*, edited with Tim Henning (2020).[^paytas-cv]
- *Plato's Pragmatism: Rethinking the Relationship between Ethics and Epistemology*, with Nicholas Baima (2021).[^paytas-cv]
