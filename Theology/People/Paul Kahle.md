# Description
Paul Kahle was a German orientalist and Old Testament scholar who taught at Giessen and Bonn and emigrated to England after being suspended from service for helping a Jewish acquaintance.[^fueck-1977] He worked mainly on the textual history of the Hebrew Bible and its ancient versions, the [[Targum|Targums]] among them.[^fueck-1977]

# Work
- The [[Masoretic Text|Masoretic text]] printed in the third edition of Rudolf Kittel's *Biblia Hebraica*, which he based on the Leningrad manuscript B 19 A of 1009 in place of the text held authoritative since 1524.[^fueck-1977]
- *The Cairo Geniza*, second edition (1959; German 1962), which gave special attention to the Qumran finds because their variants bore out his view that the Hebrew text was the end result of a long development.[^fueck-1977]
