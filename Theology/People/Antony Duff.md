# Description
Antony Duff is a philosopher of criminal law and punishment, and emeritus professor of philosophy at the University of Stirling.[^stirling-duff] He also held a part-time post at the University of Minnesota Law School.[^stirling-duff]

# Work
- "Pascal's Wager and Infinite Utilities", *Analysis* 46/2 (1986), 107–109.[^duff-1986]
- *Trials and Punishments* (1986).[^stirling-duff]
- *Punishment, Communication, and Community* (2001).[^stirling-duff]
- *Answering for Crime* (2007).[^stirling-duff]
- *The Realm of Criminal Law* (2018).[^stirling-duff]
