# Description
Lara Buchak is a philosopher at Princeton University, where she co-directs the Princeton Project in Philosophy and Religion, and earlier taught at the University of California, Berkeley.[^princeton-buchak][^berkeley-buchak] She works on decision theory and on the nature and rationality of faith.[^princeton-buchak]

# Work
- "Can it be Rational to Have Faith?", in *Probability in the Philosophy of Religion*, ed. Jake Chandler and Victoria S. Harrison (Oxford University Press, 2012), 225–248.[^buchak-2012]
- *Risk and Rationality* (Oxford University Press, 2013).[^berkeley-buchak]
- "Rational Faith and Justified Belief", in *Religious Faith and Intellectual Virtue*, ed. Laura Frances Callahan and Timothy O'Connor (Oxford University Press, 2014), 49–73.[^buchak-2014]
