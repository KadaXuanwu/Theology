# Description
Seneca the Younger was a Roman Stoic philosopher, tutor and then adviser to Nero, who ordered his death after the Pisonian conspiracy. His *Naturales Quaestiones* sets out natural explanations of weather and the sky alongside the religious readings of the same events.[^sep-seneca][^iep-seneca]

# Work
- *De Clementia*, around 55 to 56 CE.[^sep-seneca]
- *Epistulae Morales ad Lucilium*, 62 to 65 CE.[^sep-seneca]
- *Naturales Quaestiones*, 62 to 65 CE.[^sep-seneca]

# Stated Position
He names fate, providence and nature as three names for one god: "Vis illum fatum uocare, non errabis... Vis illum prouidentiam dicere, recte dices... Vis illum naturam uocare, non peccabis."[^seneca-nq-2-45] He died before Christianity was a subject anyone in Rome wrote about, and the letters between him and Paul are a fourth century forgery.
