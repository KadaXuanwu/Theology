# Description
James Dominic Rooney is a philosopher at Hong Kong Baptist University and a Dominican friar. He works on metaphysics and on the analytic defence of hell and of divine hiddenness.[^hkbu-rooney][^orcid-rooney]

# Work
- *Material Objects in Confucian and Aristotelian Metaphysics* (2022).[^orcid-rooney]
- "'Hell? Yes!' Moorean reasons to reject three objections to the possibility of damnation", *Religious Studies* (2024).[^orcid-rooney]
- "We Deserve It: An Augustinian Response to Divine Hiddenness Arguments", *New Blackfriars* (2024).[^orcid-rooney]
