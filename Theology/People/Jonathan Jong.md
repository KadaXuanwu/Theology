# Description
Jonathan Jong is a psychologist of religion who works on death anxiety and belief, and an Anglican priest. He now publishes as Jonathan Lewis-Jong.[^jong-site][^newatlantis-jong]

# Work
- *Death, Anxiety, and Religious Belief: An Existential Psychology of Religion*, with Jamin Halberstadt (2016).[^jong-site]
- *Experimenting with Religion: The New Science of Belief* (2023).[^jong-site]

# Stated Position
He describes himself as a paid up member of the Church of England and a clerk in holy orders, and gives his own reasons for that commitment while arguing that the reasons people state are rarely the causes of their belief.[^newatlantis-jong]
