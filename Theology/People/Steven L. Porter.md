# Description
Steven L. Porter is a philosopher and theologian of spiritual formation at Biola University's Talbot School of Theology and at the Martin Institute of Westmont College.[^biola-talbot][^westmont-porter][^yale-porter] He has written on epistemology and, in *Faith and Philosophy*, a defence of the doctrine of penal substitution.[^yale-porter][^porter-2004]

# Work
- "Swinburnian Atonement and the Doctrine of Penal Substitution", *Faith and Philosophy* 21/2 (2004), 228–241.[^porter-2004]
- *Restoring the Foundations of Epistemic Justification: A Direct Realist and Conceptualist Theory of Foundationalism* (Lexington Books, 2006).[^yale-porter][^ol-2006]
- *Psychology and Spiritual Formation in Dialogue*, edited with Thomas M. Crisp and Gregg A. Ten Elshof (InterVarsity Press, 2019).[^regent-porter][^ol-2019]

# Stated Position
In a 2018 interview he says: "Very early in my life (around age seven) I began to have rather undeniable (even if rare) experiences of God's presence."[^eps-2018]
