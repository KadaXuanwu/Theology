# Description
Daniel Speak is a philosopher at Loyola Marymount University in Los Angeles, where he has taught since 2007, working on free will, moral responsibility and the problem of evil.[^lmu-speak] He argues that belief cannot be a condition of salvation for everyone, since belief is not under direct voluntary control and God does not condemn people for failing at what they cannot do.[^speak-2007]

# Work
- "Salvation without belief", *Religious Studies* 43/2 (2007), 229–236.[^speak-2007]
- *The Problem of Evil* (2014).[^speak-2014]

# Stated Position
In "Salvation without belief" he writes that he is "in agreement with Pojman's conclusion that the kind of faith required for salvation in the Christian tradition need not entail propositional belief in the standard philosophical sense".[^speak-2007]
