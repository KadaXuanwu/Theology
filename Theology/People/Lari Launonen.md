# Description
Lari Launonen is a philosopher of religion at the University of Helsinki, working on what the cognitive science of religion does and does not license. He is the source of the vault's material on the suppression thesis and on non-belief as self deception.[^helsinki-launonen]

# Work
- "Debunking Arguments Gain Little from Cognitive Science of Religion", *Zygon* (2021).[^helsinki-launonen]
- *Natural Religion in Science and Theology*, doctoral thesis, University of Helsinki (2022).[^helsinki-launonen]
- "Non-belief as self-deception?", *Religious Studies* 61/2 (2025), 263–279.[^launonen-2025]
