# Description
John Henry Bernard was an Irish biblical scholar and churchman who ended his career as Provost of Trinity College Dublin, having been Archbishop of Dublin before that.[^dib-bernard] The vault cites his posthumous commentary on John for the reading of the perfect in John 3:18 and for the relation of verse 19 to it.[^bernard-1928]

# Work
- *The Pastoral Epistles*, Cambridge Greek Testament for Schools and Colleges (1899).[^dib-bernard]
- *The Odes of Solomon*, edited with J. Rendel Harris (1912).[^dib-bernard]
- *Dogma and Criticism* (1920).[^dib-bernard]
- *A Critical and Exegetical Commentary on the Gospel According to St. John*, International Critical Commentary, ed. A. H. McNeile, 2 vols (T. & T. Clark, 1928).[^bernard-1928]

# Stated Position
None found in print. The *Dictionary of Irish Biography* characterises his churchmanship as liberal and high, which is its biographer's judgement rather than a statement of his own.[^dib-bernard]

[^dib-bernard]: ["Bernard, John Henry"](https://www.dib.ie/biography/bernard-john-henry-a0628), *Dictionary of Irish Biography*, Royal Irish Academy. He was Archbishop King's Lecturer in Divinity from 1888, Dean of St Patrick's from 1902, Bishop of Ossory from 1911, Archbishop of Dublin from 1915 and Provost of Trinity from 1919. Wikipedia gives his birth instead as 27 November 1860 at Raniganj, which is the date consistent with its own statement that he died aged 66; the conflict is unresolved.
