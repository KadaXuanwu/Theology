# Description
J. H. Bernard was an Irish churchman and scholar: Dean of St Patrick's Cathedral from 1902, Bishop of Ossory from 1911, Archbishop of Dublin from 1915, and Provost of Trinity College Dublin from 1919 until his death.[^tcd-bernard] His two volume commentary on John for the International Critical Commentary was published after his death, edited by A. H. McNeile.[^bernard-1928]

# Work
- *The Pilgrimage of S. Silvia of Aquitania to the Holy Places*, translation (Palestine Pilgrims' Text Society, 1896).[^penn-bernard]
- *Dogma and Criticism* (Longmans, Green, 1920).[^bernard-1920]
- *A Critical and Exegetical Commentary on the Gospel According to St. John*, 2 vols (T&T Clark, 1928).[^bernard-1928]

# Stated Position
"Speaking for myself, I believe that the evidence for the objective and historical fact of our Lord's Resurrection is not only sufficient but that in some respects it makes a stronger appeal than it did before it had been subjected to the laborious analysis of modern critics."[^bernard-1920]
