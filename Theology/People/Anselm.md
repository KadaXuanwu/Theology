# Description
Anselm of Canterbury was a monk of Bec who became Archbishop of Canterbury in 1093. *Cur Deus Homo* is where the satisfaction account of the atonement is set out, and the *Proslogion* is where the ontological argument first appears.[^sep-anselm][^iep-anselm]

# Work
- *Monologion* (1075 to 1076).[^sep-anselm]
- *Proslogion* (1077 to 1078).[^sep-anselm]
- *Cur Deus Homo* (1095 to 1098).[^sep-anselm]

# Stated Position
His own name for his method is faith seeking understanding, fides quaerens intellectum, which was the *Proslogion*'s original title.[^iep-anselm]
