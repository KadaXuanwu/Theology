# Description
Elisa Järnefelt studies whether people who reject creation still read nature as made on purpose. Her doctorate is in the study of religion at Helsinki, and the experimental work behind it was done at Boston University with Deborah Kelemen.[^jarnefelt-site]

# Work
- *Created by Some Being*, doctoral thesis, University of Helsinki (2013).[^jarnefelt-site]
- "The divided mind of a disbeliever", with Caitlin Canfield and Deborah Kelemen, *Cognition* 140 (2015), 72–88.[^jarnefelt-2015]
- "Beneath the Surface: A Critique of the Common Survey Model in the Study of Nonreligion", *Secularism and Nonreligion* 9 (2020).[^jarnefelt-site]
