# Description
Amnon Ben-Tor was an archaeologist at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, where he held the Yigael Yadin chair in the archaeology of the Land of Israel until he retired.[^bar-ben-tor][^jns-ben-tor] He directed the renewed excavations at Hazor.[^bar-ben-tor]

# Work
- "Excavating Hazor, Part Two: Did the Israelites Destroy the Canaanite City?", with Maria Teresa Rubiato (1999).[^bhd-ben-tor]
- "Who Destroyed Canaanite Hazor?" (2013).[^bhd-ben-tor]
- *Hazor: Canaanite Metropolis, Israelite City* (2016).[^armstrong-ben-tor]

# Stated Position
In 1996 he told the Associated Press: "Nobody can prove to me the story in Joshua is entirely fiction."[^ap-1996]
