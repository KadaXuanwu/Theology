# Description
Josh McDowell is a Christian author and speaker who joined the staff of Campus Crusade for Christ.[^sightline-mcdowell] His books include *Evidence That Demands a Verdict* and *More Than a Carpenter*.[^sightline-mcdowell]

# Work
- *Evidence That Demands a Verdict*, revised edition (1979).[^mcdowell-1979]
- *More Than a Carpenter* (1977).[^loc-mcdowell]

# Stated Position
In a profile by the Christian Broadcasting Network he dates his conversion: "that December the 19th at 8:30 at night, I became a Christian."[^cbn-mcdowell]
