# Description
Robert J. Miller is Rosenberger Professor of Religious Studies and Christian Thought at Juniata College in Pennsylvania.[^lutterworth-miller] He works on the Gospels, early Christian gospels outside the New Testament and the use of prophecy in the New Testament, and edits the Westar Institute's magazine *The Fourth R*.[^westar-miller]

# Work
- *The Complete Gospels*, editor (Polebridge Press, 1992; 4th edn 2010).[^pw-complete][^lutterworth-miller]
- *The Jesus Seminar and Its Critics* (1999).[^westar-miller]
- *Born Divine* (2003).[^westar-miller]
- *Helping Jesus Fulfill Prophecy* (Cascade Books, 2016; Lutterworth Press, 2016).[^loc-miller][^westar-miller] Chapter 11 treats the fulfilment of prophecy in John. It calls three details of the crucifixion there "almost certainly narrative fictions, created by John out of the very scriptures they fulfill" (p. 195).[^miller-2016]
