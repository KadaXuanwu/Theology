# Description
Jeffrey Koperski is a philosopher at Saginaw Valley State University who works on physics, divine action and the laws of nature. He writes the Stanford Encyclopedia entry on teleological arguments with Del Ratzsch.[^svsu-koperski][^sep-teleological]

# Work
- *The Physics of Theism: God, Physics, and the Philosophy of Science* (2015).[^henry-koperski]
- *Divine Action, Determinism, and the Laws of Nature* (2019).[^henry-koperski]
- "Teleological Arguments for God's Existence", with Del Ratzsch, *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2005, revised 2023).[^sep-teleological]
