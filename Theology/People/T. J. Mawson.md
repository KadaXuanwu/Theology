# Description
T. J. Mawson is professor of the philosophy of religion at Oxford and a fellow of St Peter's College, where he has taught since 1998.[^oxford-mawson] He argues that the cultural spread of religious belief bears on claims to religious knowledge, and cuts the same way against the irreligious.[^mawson-2009]

# Work
- *Belief in God: An Introduction to the Philosophy of Religion* (2005).[^oxford-mawson]
- "Mill's Argument against Religious Knowledge", *Religious Studies* 45/4 (2009), 417–434.[^mawson-2009]
- "The Rationality of Classical Theism and Its Demographics", in *Scientific Approaches to the Philosophy of Religion*, ed. Yujin Nagasawa (Palgrave Macmillan, 2012), 184–201.[^mawson-2012]
- *The Divine Attributes* (2018).[^oxford-mawson]

# Stated Position
Asked in an interview whether he is religious, he answers "I am a Christian".[^mawson-interview]
