# Description
Niels Peter Lemche is a scholar of the Old Testament who taught at Aarhus University and then became professor of Old Testament exegesis at the University of Copenhagen.[^ku-lemche] He founded the *Scandinavian Journal of the Old Testament* and served as its chief editor.[^ku-lemche]

# Work
- *Early Israel* (1985).[^openlibrary-early-israel]
- *The Israelites in History and Tradition* (1998).[^openlibrary-israelites]
- "On the Problems of Reconstructing Pre-Hellenistic Israelite (Palestinian) History" (2001).[^lemche-2001]

# Stated Position
On the inscriptions that name Israel and Judah, he writes that the two names "refer to political structures that really existed".[^lemche-2001] On what one of them does for the biblical account beside it: "The Mesha inscription does not make 2 Kings 3 a reliable historical source, nor changes its basic genre."[^lemche-2001] On the Old Testament as a basis for pre-Hellenistic history: "It is simply an invented history with only a few referents to things that really happened or existed."[^lemche-2001]
