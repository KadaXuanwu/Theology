# Description
Stewart Guthrie is an anthropologist, emeritus at Fordham University. *Faces in the Clouds* is the case that religion is a by-product of anthropomorphism, the habit of reading human agency into the world.[^fordham-guthrie][^loc-guthrie]

# Work
- "A Cognitive Theory of Religion", *Current Anthropology* (1980).[^fordham-guthrie]
- *A Japanese New Religion: Risshō Kōsei-kai in a Mountain Hamlet* (1988).[^loc-guthrie]
- *Faces in the Clouds: A New Theory of Religion* (1993).[^fordham-guthrie]
