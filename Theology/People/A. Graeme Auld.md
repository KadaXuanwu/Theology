# Description
A. Graeme Auld is Professor Emeritus of Hebrew Bible at the University of Edinburgh, where he taught at New College and was Dean of Divinity and Principal of New College.[^edinburgh-auld][^gnd-auld] He holds that Samuel and Kings and the books of Chronicles grew independently from one older narrative about the house of David and the house of Yahweh.[^auld-2017]

# Work
- *Kings without Privilege* (T&T Clark, 1994), which he later described as arguing "that the books of Samuel–Kings should no longer be privileged as the main source of Chronicles".[^auld-2017]
- A commentary on I and II Samuel for the Old Testament Library (2011).[^edinburgh-auld]
- *Life in Kings: Reshaping the Royal Story in the Hebrew Bible* (SBL Press, 2017), which names that shared narrative the Book of Two Houses.[^auld-2017]
- *Seeing David Double* (2023), a collection of essays.[^edinburgh-auld]
