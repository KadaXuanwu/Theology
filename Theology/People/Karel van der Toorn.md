# Description
Karel van der Toorn is a scholar of ancient Near Eastern religion and the Hebrew Bible, and Faculty Professor of Religion and Society at the University of Amsterdam.[^amacad-vandertoorn] He earlier taught ancient religions at Utrecht and Leiden and later served as president of the University of Amsterdam.[^amacad-vandertoorn]

# Work
- *Dictionary of Deities and Demons in the Bible* (1995), as managing editor.[^amacad-vandertoorn]
- *Family Religion in Babylonia, Syria and Israel* (1996).[^thetorah-vandertoorn]
- *Scribal Culture and the Making of the Hebrew Bible* (2007).[^amacad-vandertoorn][^thetorah-vandertoorn]
- *Becoming Diaspora Jews: Behind the Story of Elephantine* (2019).[^thetorah-vandertoorn]
