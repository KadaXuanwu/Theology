# Description
Charity Anderson is a philosopher at Baylor University who works on evidence and on divine hiddenness. Her position is that the evidential force of hiddenness is weaker than the argument needs.[^baylor-anderson]

# Work
- "Divine Hiddenness: Defeated Evidence", *Royal Institute of Philosophy Supplement* 81 (2017), 119–132.[^baylor-anderson]
- "Divine hiddenness: an evidential argument", *Philosophical Perspectives* 35 (2021), 5–22.[^baylor-anderson]
- *Divine Hiddenness and Evidence for God* (2025).[^oup-anderson]
