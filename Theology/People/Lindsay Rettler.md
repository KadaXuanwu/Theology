# Description
Lindsay Rettler is a philosopher at the University of Wyoming working in epistemology, moral psychology and applied ethics.[^uwyo-rettler][^uw-bioethics-rettler] The vault cites her for grounding doxastic blame in indirect reflective control rather than in direct control over what one believes.[^rettler-2018a][^rettler-2018b]

# Work
- "Faith, Belief, and Control", *American Philosophical Quarterly* 55/1 (2018), 95–109.[^rettler-2018a]
- "In Defense of Doxastic Blame", *Synthese* 195/5 (2018), 2205–2226.[^rettler-2018b]
- "Epistemic Duty and Implicit Bias", in *Epistemic Duties: New Arguments, New Angles* (2020).[^uwyo-rettler]
