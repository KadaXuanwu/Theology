# Description
Karl Rahner was a German Jesuit theologian who taught at Innsbruck, Munich and Münster, and served as a peritus at the Second Vatican Council.[^ndb-rahner] The vault cites him for the criterion that what matters is not having heard the gospel but having been existentially confronted with it.[^bullivant-2011]

# Work
- *Geist in Welt*, translated as *Spirit in the World* (1939).[^ndb-rahner]
- *Hörer des Wortes* (Kösel, 1941), translated as *Hearers of the Word*.[^rahner-1941]
- *Theological Investigations*, 23 volumes in English translation (1961 to 1992).[^rahner-1961]
- *Grundkurs des Glaubens*, translated as *Foundations of Christian Faith* (1976).[^ndb-rahner]

# Stated Position
On the term he is best known for: "'Anonymous Christianity' means that a person lives in the grace of God and attains salvation outside of explicitly constituted Christianity."[^rahner-dialogue]

He entered the Society of Jesus in 1922 and was ordained in 1932. He qualified as a lecturer at Innsbruck in 1937 and taught there until the Nazi authorities abolished the theology faculty in July 1938, and at the church faculty that carried on until 1939; he held a full chair at Innsbruck from 1949.
