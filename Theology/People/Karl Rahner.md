# Description
Karl Rahner was a German Jesuit theologian who taught at Innsbruck, Munich and Münster, and served as a peritus at the Second Vatican Council.[^britannica-rahner] The vault cites him for the criterion that what matters is not having heard the gospel but having been existentially confronted with it.[^bullivant-2011]

# Work
- *Geist in Welt*, translated as *Spirit in the World* (1939).[^britannica-rahner]
- *Hörer des Wortes*, translated as *Hearers of the Word* (1941).[^britannica-rahner]
- *Theological Investigations*, 23 volumes in English translation (1961 to 1992).[^britannica-rahner]
- *Grundkurs des Glaubens*, translated as *Foundations of Christian Faith* (1976).[^britannica-rahner]

# Stated Position
On the term he is best known for: "'Anonymous Christianity' means that a person lives in the grace of God and attains salvation outside of explicitly constituted Christianity."[^rahner-dialogue]
