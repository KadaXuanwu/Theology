# Description
Nicholas Hadsell is a philosopher at West Virginia University who studied for a doctorate at Baylor University with a dissertation on parental rights.[^hadsell-2026][^hadsell-cv] He argues that "God is authoritative over us because he is our divine, causal parent".[^hadsell-2025]

# Work
- "Eternal Hell and Impaired Agency: A Reply to Marilyn Adams", *Heythrop Journal* 63/5 (2022), 899–906.[^hadsell-2022]
- "Divine authority as divine parenthood", *Religious Studies* 61/4 (2025), 828–849.[^hadsell-2025]
- "Why (and How) States Protect Children", *Journal of Ethics and Social Philosophy* 31/1 (2026), 81–106.[^hadsell-2026]

# Stated Position
"While I am a Christian and have developed my view of divine authority with the Christian tradition in mind, what I say here applies to any tradition that sees God as holy, our creator, and our ultimate authority" (pp. 829–830).[^hadsell-2025]
