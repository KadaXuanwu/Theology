# Description
A. N. Sherwin-White was a Roman historian, Fellow and Tutor of St John's College, Oxford, and a Reader in the university.[^whoswho-sherwin-white][^jrs-sherwin-white] He worked on Roman citizenship, public law and administration.[^jrs-sherwin-white]

# Work
- *The Roman Citizenship* (1939; 2nd ed. 1973).[^jrs-sherwin-white]
- *Roman Society and Roman Law in the New Testament* (Clarendon Press, 1963), the Sarum Lectures of 1960 to 1961.[^hbz-sherwin-white][^jrs-sherwin-white]
- *The Letters of Pliny: A Historical and Social Commentary* (1966).[^hbz-sherwin-white-pliny][^jrs-sherwin-white]

# Stated Position
No published statement of his own position on the truth of Christianity was found.
