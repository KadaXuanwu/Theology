# Description
Larry W. Hurtado was a New Testament scholar and Professor of New Testament Language, Literature and Theology at the University of Edinburgh, where he founded the Centre for the Study of Christian Origins.[^edinburgh-hurtado][^gnd-hurtado] He worked on early devotion to Jesus and on the earliest Christian manuscripts.[^hurtado-about]

# Work
- *Lord Jesus Christ: Devotion to Jesus in Earliest Christianity* (Eerdmans, 2003).[^hbz-hurtado-lord]
- *The Earliest Christian Artifacts: Manuscripts and Christian Origins* (Eerdmans, 2006).[^hbz-hurtado-artifacts]
- "The Early New Testament Papyri: A Survey of Their Significance" (2012), reprinted in *Texts and Artefacts: Selected Essays on Textual Criticism and Early Christian Manuscripts* (Bloomsbury T&T Clark, 2018).[^hurtado-2012][^hbz-hurtado-texts][^loc-hurtado-texts]
