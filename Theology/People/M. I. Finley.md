# Description
M. I. Finley was an ancient historian, Professor of Ancient History at the University of Cambridge and Master of Darwin College.[^whittaker-1997] He taught at Rutgers University before he moved to England.[^dbcs-finley]

# Work
- *The World of Odysseus* (1954).[^dbcs-finley]
- "The Origins of Christianity", *The New York Review of Books*, 20 August 1964.[^finley-1964]
- *Aspects of Antiquity: Discoveries and Controversies* (1968).[^dbcs-finley]
- *The Ancient Economy* (1973), the Sather Lectures.[^dbcs-finley]

# Stated Position
No published statement of his own position on the truth of Christianity was found.
