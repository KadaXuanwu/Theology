# Description
Craig S. Keener is a New Testament scholar at Asbury Theological Seminary who writes very large commentaries and works on the credibility of miracle reports.[^asbury-keener] His two volume commentary on John and his study of miracle accounts are both what the vault cites him for.[^keener-2003][^keener-2011]

# Work
- *The Gospel of John: A Commentary*, two volumes (2003).[^keener-2003]
- *Miracles: The Credibility of the New Testament Accounts*, two volumes (2011).[^keener-2011]
- *Acts: An Exegetical Commentary*, four volumes (2012 to 2015).[^keener-site]
- *Christobiography: Memory, History, and the Reliability of the Gospels* (2019).[^keener-site]

# Stated Position
He describes himself as a former atheist and says that witnessing a miracle would not have converted him instantly but "would have gotten my attention and turned me into an inquirer".[^keener-ct]
