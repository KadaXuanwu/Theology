# Description
John Van Seters was a biblical scholar and University Distinguished Professor Emeritus at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.[^cascade-2018][^gnd-vanseters] Jim Davila wrote at his death that he was "known best for his revisionist approach to Pentateuchal research".[^davila-2025]

# Work
- *The Pentateuch: A Social-Science Commentary* (Sheffield Academic Press, 1999; second edition, Bloomsbury T&T Clark, 2015).[^cascade-2018][^hbz-pentateuch]
- *The Yahwist: A Historian of Israelite Origins* (2013).[^cascade-2018][^hbz-yahwist]
- *My Life and Career as a Biblical Scholar* (Cascade Books, 2018), a memoir.[^cascade-2018]
