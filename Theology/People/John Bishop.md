# Description
John Bishop is an emeritus professor of philosophy at the University of Auckland in New Zealand, working on the philosophy of religion and of action.[^auckland-2026][^aap-bishop] He defends faith as a "doxastic venture", believing beyond what the evidence settles. He wrote the *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* entry on faith, later revised with Daniel J. McKaughan.[^bishop-2002][^bishop-2010][^bishop-mckaughan-2023]

# Work
- "Faith as doxastic venture", *Religious Studies* 38/4 (2002), 471–487.[^bishop-2002]
- *Believing by Faith: An Essay in the Epistemology and Ethics of Religious Belief* (Clarendon Press, 2007).[^bishop-mckaughan-2023]
- "Faith", *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (first published 2010), with Daniel J. McKaughan from the 2022 revision.[^bishop-2010][^bishop-mckaughan-2023]
- With Ken Perszyk, *God, Purpose, and Reality: A Euteleological Understanding of Theism* (Oxford University Press, 2023).[^bishop-mckaughan-2023]
