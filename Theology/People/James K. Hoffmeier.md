# Description
James K. Hoffmeier is emeritus professor of Old Testament and Near Eastern archaeology at Trinity Evangelical Divinity School, with a doctorate in Egyptian studies from the University of Toronto.[^kregel-hoffmeier][^juc-hoffmeier] He taught at Wheaton College and then at Trinity.[^juc-hoffmeier]

# Work
- *Israel in Egypt: The Evidence for the Authenticity of the Exodus Tradition* (1997).[^ol-hoffmeier]
- *Ancient Israel in Sinai: The Evidence for the Authenticity of the Wilderness Tradition* (2005).[^juc-hoffmeier]
- "What Is the Biblical Date for the Exodus? A Response to Bryant Wood" (2007).[^hoffmeier-2007]

# Stated Position
He concludes that "the biblical and archaeological data point to Joshua destroying the LB IIB city of Hazor".[^hoffmeier-2007] On the date of the exodus he writes that for now the biblical and archaeological data "converge nicely in the 13th century", and that he would shift his position if new evidence supported the 15th century.[^hoffmeier-2007]
