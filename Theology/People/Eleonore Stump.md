# Description
Eleonore Stump is a philosopher who has held the Robert J. Henle chair of philosophy at Saint Louis University since 1992.[^stump-cv] She works on Aquinas and on the problem of suffering.[^stump-cv]

# Work
- *Aquinas* (Routledge, 2003).[^stump-cv]
- *Wandering in Darkness: Narrative and the Problem of Suffering* (Oxford University Press, 2010).[^stump-cv]
- "The Problem of Evil and the History of Peoples: Think Amalek", in *Divine Evil? The Moral Character of the God of Abraham*, ed. Michael Bergmann, Michael J. Murray and Michael C. Rea (Oxford University Press, 2011), 179–197.[^stump-2011]
