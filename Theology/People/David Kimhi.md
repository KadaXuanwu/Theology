# Description
David Kimhi (ReDaK, surnamed Maistre Petit) was a Provençal grammarian and Bible commentator who wrote on Genesis, the Prophets, the Psalms and Chronicles.[^je-kimhi] His commentaries read the text through grammar and the literal meaning, and they were used by Christian as well as Jewish readers in the Middle Ages.[^je-kimhi]

# Work
- *Miklol*, a Hebrew grammar, first printed in Italy in 1525.[^je-kimhi]
- *Sefer ha-Shorashim* ("Book of Roots"), a dictionary of the Bible, originally the second part of the *Miklol*, first printed in Italy before 1480.[^je-kimhi]
- Commentaries on the Earlier Prophets (first printed 1482), the Later Prophets (first printed 1485), the Psalms (first printed 1477) and Chronicles.[^je-kimhi]
- *Teshubot la-Noẓerim*, his polemic against Christianity, taken from his Psalms commentary and published separately after censors cut it from later printings.[^je-kimhi]

# Stated Position
On the Christian reading of [Psalm 2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm%202&version=NIV) he wrote, in the English of R. G. Finch: "And the Nazarenes interpret it of Jesus; and the verse they adduce by way of proof and make a support of their error is really their stumbling-block".[^kimhi-psalms] He adds that "of the Godhead it is unfitting (to speak of) father and son; because the Godhead cannot be divided".[^kimhi-psalms] The Jewish Encyclopedia calls his Psalms commentary "especially noted for its polemics against Christianity".[^je-kimhi]
