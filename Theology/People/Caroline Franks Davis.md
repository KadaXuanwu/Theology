# Description
Caroline Franks Davis is an institutional researcher who has published on institutional research and the philosophy of religion, including a book on the evidence of religious experience.[^franks-davis-1989] Richard Jones and Jerome Gellman report that she tries "to show that mystical experiences really support a 'broad theism'".[^jones-gellman-sep]

# Work
- *The Evidential Force of Religious Experience* (Clarendon Press, 1989).[^franks-davis-1989]
