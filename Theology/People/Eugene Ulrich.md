# Description
Eugene Ulrich is a scholar of the Dead Sea Scrolls who was John A. O'Brien Professor of Hebrew Scripture and Theology at the University of Notre Dame until he retired.[^bishop-2019] He was chief editor of the biblical scrolls in the Scrolls International Publication Project and a translator of the New Revised Standard Version.[^bishop-2019][^bas-ulrich]

# Work
- *The Dead Sea Scrolls and the Origins of the Bible* (Brill, 1999).[^brill-ulrich]
- "The Notion and Definition of Canon", in *The Canon Debate*, ed. Lee Martin McDonald and James A. Sanders (Hendrickson, 2002), 21–35. In it he argues for defining a [[Canon|canon]] as a closed list, and concludes that "talk of an open canon is confusing and counterproductive".[^zehnder-2012]
