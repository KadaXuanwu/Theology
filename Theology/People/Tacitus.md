# Description
Publius Cornelius Tacitus was a Roman historian, suffect consul in 97 and proconsul of Asia around 112. *Annals* 15.44 is the earliest non-Christian account of the execution of Christ under Pontius Pilate.[^britannica-tacitus]

# Work
- *Agricola* (98).[^britannica-tacitus]
- *Germania* (98).[^britannica-tacitus]
- *Histories*, covering AD 69 to 96.[^britannica-tacitus]
- *Annals*, covering AD 14 to 68.[^tacitus-annals]

# Stated Position
He calls Christianity a deadly superstition and Judaea the first source of the evil, in *Annals* 15.44.[^tacitus-annals] The dates of his own life are scholarly estimates and every source hedges them.
