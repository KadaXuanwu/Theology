# Description
Halvor Kvandal is an associate professor in the Department of Culture, Religion and Social Studies at the University of South-Eastern Norway, with a PhD from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology.[^usn-kvandal][^nva-thesis] He works on what the cognitive science of religion implies for whether belief in God is justified.[^nva-thesis]

# Work
- *Prone to Believe in God: The Cognitive Science of Religion and its Normative Implications for Theist Religion*, PhD thesis, Norwegian University of Science and Technology (2019).[^nva-thesis]
- "The God-Faculty Dilemma: Challenges for Reformed Epistemology in the Light of Cognitive Science", *International Journal of Philosophy and Theology* 81/4 (2020), 404–422.[^kvandal-2020]
- *God Naturalized: Epistemological Reflections on Theistic Belief in Light of the New Science of Religion* (Springer, 2022).[^kvandal-2022]
- "Why the Debunking Threat Won't Go Away", *International Journal for Philosophy of Religion* 94/3 (2023), 299–316.[^kvandal-2023]

# Stated Position
His thesis summary supposes that the cognitive science of religion is "true or at least approximately true" and concludes: "The theistic beliefs subjects are prone to form are therefore unjustified."[^nva-thesis]
