# Description
Origen was a Christian exegete and theologian who used the allegorical method in his biblical commentaries.[^sep-origen] He worked in Alexandria and later in Caesarea, and prepared the Hexapla, an edition of the Old Testament in Hebrew and Greek.[^sep-origen][^ce-hexapla]

# Work
- *On First Principles*, which Mark J. Edwards calls "his major surviving work, a synthesis of scriptural teaching".[^sep-origen] It survives complete only in the Latin of Rufinus.[^sep-origen]
- The Hexapla, his edition of the Old Testament in Hebrew and Greek. The Catholic Encyclopedia calls it "the most colossal critical production of antiquity".[^ce-hexapla]
- *Against Celsus* (Contra Celsum), eight books answering the pagan writer Celsus. It survives in the original Greek, and he was over sixty when he wrote it.[^ce-origen][^sep-origen]
- Commentaries on Matthew and John. Eight books on Matthew and nine on John remain in Greek, and part of the Matthew commentary in an anonymous Latin translation.[^ce-origen]

# Stated Position
At the head of *On First Principles* he writes that "that alone is to be accepted as truth which differs in no respect from ecclesiastical and apostolic tradition."[^origen-principiis]
