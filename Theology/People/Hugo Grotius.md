# Description
Hugo Grotius was a Dutch jurist and scholar who also wrote theology, and who served as Attorney General of Holland and later as Swedish ambassador in Paris.[^miller-sep][^onuma-britannica] His defence of the doctrine of [[Satisfaction|satisfaction]] argues that Christ bore the penalty for sin so that people could be freed "without prejudice to the exhibition of the divine justice".[^grotius-1617]

# Work
- *Defensio fidei catholicae de satisfactione Christi adversus Faustum Socinum* (1617).[^magliano-tromp-2017][^grotius-1617]
- *De iure belli ac pacis* (1625).[^miller-sep]
- *De veritate religionis Christianae* (1627).[^onuma-britannica]

# Stated Position
In the *Defensio* he states the doctrine he defends as the church's: that Christ should, "by bearing the most severe tortures, and a bloody and ignominious death, pay the penalty for our sins, in order that without prejudice to the exhibition of the divine justice, we might be liberated" (ch. 1).[^grotius-1617]
