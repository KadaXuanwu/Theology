# Description
David Friedrich Strauss was a German Protestant theologian and writer.[^graf-2013] He analysed the Gospels as mythical narratives in *Das Leben Jesu*, a book that ended his hopes of an academic career.[^graf-2013]

# Work
- *Das Leben Jesu, kritisch bearbeitet* (1835–1836), translated into English by George Eliot as *The Life of Jesus Critically Examined* (1846; second edition 1892).[^graf-2013][^strauss-1892]
- *Die christliche Glaubenslehre in ihrer geschichtlichen Entwicklung und im Kampfe mit der modernen Wissenschaft dargestellt* (1840–1841).[^graf-2013]
- *Der alte und der neue Glaube* (1872), English translation by Mathilde Blind as *The Old Faith and the New* (1873).[^graf-2013][^strauss-1873]

# Stated Position
In *The Old Faith and the New*, under the heading "Are We Still Christians?", he wrote at p. 107: "we must acknowledge we are no longer Christians." He added that this did not mean giving up religion altogether.[^strauss-1873]
