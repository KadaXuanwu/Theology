# Description
Arthur Peacocke was a biochemist and Anglican priest who worked to bring science and theology into dialogue, founding the Science and Religion Forum and the Society of Ordained Scientists.[^ca-2007] He taught at Birmingham and Oxford, was Dean of Clare College, Cambridge, and then directed the Ian Ramsey Centre at Oxford.[^ca-2007][^templeton][^gifford]

# Work
- *Science and the Christian Experiment* (1971).[^ca-2007]
- *Intimations of Reality: Critical Realism in Science and Religion* (1984).[^ca-2007]
- *Theology for a Scientific Age: Being and Becoming, Natural, Divine, and Human* (1993).[^ca-2007]
- He received the Templeton Prize in 2001.[^ca-2007][^templeton]

# Stated Position
No statement in his own words was checked against its source in this run. His obituary notice reports that he "did not believe that science and theology were incompatible".[^ca-2007]
