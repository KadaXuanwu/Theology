# Description
John Hick was a philosopher of religion who held the H. G. Wood chair at Birmingham and the Danforth chair at Claremont. He built the soul making answer to the problem of evil, and the pluralist account on which the religions are different human responses to one transcendent reality.[^the-hick][^iep-hick]

# Work
- *Evil and the God of Love* (1966).[^the-hick]
- *God and the Universe of Faiths* (1973).[^iep-hick]
- *An Interpretation of Religion: Human Responses to the Transcendent* (1989).[^iep-hick]

# Stated Position
He read the incarnation as metaphor rather than literal fact and called that the true Christian doctrine of the incarnation.[^hick-pluralist]
