# Description
Eta Linnemann was a German New Testament scholar who took her exegetical method from [[Rudolf Bultmann]], held a chair at Braunschweig's Teachers' College and an honorary professorship at Marburg.[^yarbrough-1997][^gnd-linnemann] After an evangelical conversion she renounced in print the critical reading of the Bible she had been trained in, retired early and taught at a Bible institute in Batu, Indonesia.[^yarbrough-1997]

# Work
- *Historical Criticism of the Bible: Methodology or Ideology?* (Baker, 1990), translated by Robert W. Yarbrough from the German of 1986.[^yarbrough-1997]
- *Is There a Synoptic Problem?* (Baker, 1992), a monograph on the [[Synoptic Problem]], published in German the same year.[^yarbrough-1997]

# Stated Position
In *Historical Criticism of the Bible* she wrote: "One can no more be a little historical-critical than a little pregnant."[^linnemann-1990]
