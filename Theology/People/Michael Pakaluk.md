# Description
Michael Pakaluk is a philosopher at the Catholic University of America and a member of the Pontifical Academy of St Thomas Aquinas. He works on Aristotle's ethics and on the gospels.[^cua-pakaluk][^loc-pakaluk]

# Work
- *Aristotle's Nicomachean Ethics: An Introduction* (2005).[^cua-pakaluk]
- *The Memoirs of St. Peter: A New Translation of the Gospel according to Mark* (2019).[^cua-pakaluk]
- *Mary's Voice in the Gospel according to John* (2021).[^cua-pakaluk]
