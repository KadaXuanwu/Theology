# Description
Stephen Bullivant is Professor of Theology and the Sociology of Religion at St Mary's University, Twickenham, and directs its Benedict XVI Centre for Religion and Society.[^stmarys-bullivant] He works in two registers, Catholic dogmatic theology and survey-based sociology of unbelief, and the vault cites him in the first, as its history of what the church has taught about inculpable ignorance.[^bullivant-2011]

# Work
- "Sine Culpa? Vatican II and Inculpable Ignorance", *Theological Studies* 72/1 (2011), 70–86.[^bullivant-2011]
- *The Salvation of Atheists and Catholic Dogmatic Theology* (Oxford University Press, 2012).[^stmarys-bullivant]
- *The Oxford Handbook of Atheism*, edited with Michael Ruse (Oxford University Press, 2013).[^stmarys-bullivant]
- *Mass Exodus: Catholic Disaffiliation in Britain and America since Vatican II* (Oxford University Press, 2019).[^stmarys-bullivant]
- *Nonverts: The Making of Ex-Christian America* (Oxford University Press, 2022).[^stmarys-bullivant]

# Stated Position
None found in print. Secondary sources describe him as a convert to Catholicism, but no first-person statement was opened in a professionally published source.
