# Description
Stephen Bullivant is a Catholic theologian and sociologist of religion at St Mary's University, Twickenham, where he directs the Benedict XVI Centre for Religion and Society.[^stmarys-bullivant] His books treat atheism, disaffiliation from the churches, and what Catholic teaching says about the salvation of unbelievers.[^stmarys-bullivant]

# Work
- "Sine Culpa? Vatican II and Inculpable Ignorance", *Theological Studies* 72/1 (2011), 70–86.[^bullivant-2011]
- *The Salvation of Atheists and Catholic Dogmatic Theology* (Oxford University Press, 2012).[^stmarys-bullivant]
- *Mass Exodus: Catholic Disaffiliation in Britain and America since Vatican II* (Oxford University Press, 2019).[^stmarys-bullivant]
- *Nonverts: The Making of Ex-Christian America* (Oxford University Press, 2022).[^seen-unseen-bullivant]

# Stated Position
He describes beginning his theology studies "as a Bertrand Russell-inspired atheist", and says he "was baptized and received into the Catholic Church while a grad student in 2008".[^cwr-bullivant-2019]
