# Description
Nicholas J. Healy Jr. is a theologian at the Pontifical John Paul II Institute in Washington and editor of the North American edition of *Communio*.[^jpii-healy] The vault cites his answer to [[Ralph Martin]] on how much Vatican II changed about the salvation of the unevangelised.[^healy-2015]

# Work
- *The Eschatology of Hans Urs von Balthasar: Being as Communion* (Oxford University Press, 2005).[^jpii-healy]
- *Freedom, Truth, and Human Dignity*, with David L. Schindler (Eerdmans, 2015).[^jpii-healy]
- "Vatican II and the Catholicity of Salvation: A Response to Ralph Martin", *Communio: International Catholic Review* 42 (2015), 36–60.[^healy-2015]
- *Ressourcement after Vatican II*, co-edited (Ignatius Press, 2019).[^jpii-healy]

# Stated Position
None found in print, separately from the argument of the 2015 article.
