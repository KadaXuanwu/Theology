# Description
Herman Philipse is a philosopher, emeritus at Utrecht, who held a chair at Leiden from 1985 to 2003. *God in the Age of Science?* argues that religious belief fails as an explanation on the standards used everywhere else.[^utrecht-philipse][^leiden-philipse]

# Work
- *Atheïstisch manifest* (1995).[^utrecht-philipse]
- *Heidegger's Philosophy of Being: A Critical Interpretation* (1998).[^utrecht-philipse]
- *God in the Age of Science? A Critique of Religious Reason* (2012).[^utrecht-philipse]
- *Reason and Religion: Evaluating and Explaining Belief in Gods* (2022).[^philipse-2022]

# Stated Position
None found in his own first person words. The publisher's abstract for *Reason and Religion* ends by saying universal atheism is the final conclusion, which is a summary of the book's argument rather than a statement about himself.[^philipse-2022]
