# Description
Mark C. Murphy is a philosopher who taught at Georgetown University from 1995 and moved to the University of Notre Dame in 2026.[^nd-murphy] In philosophy of religion he works on how God explains moral norms and on the norms by which God himself acts.[^nd-murphy]

# Work
- *God and Moral Law: On the Theistic Explanation of Morality* (Oxford University Press, 2011).[^murphy-cv]
- "God Beyond Justice", in *Divine Evil? The Moral Character of the God of Abraham*, ed. Michael Bergmann, Michael J. Murray and Michael C. Rea (Oxford University Press, 2011), 150–167.[^murphy-2011]
- *God's Own Ethics: Norms of Divine Agency and the Argument from Evil* (Oxford University Press, 2017).[^murphy-cv]
