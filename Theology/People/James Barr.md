# Description
James Barr was a scholar of the Hebrew Bible and of biblical language who held the Regius chair of Hebrew at Oxford and later taught at Vanderbilt. His Gifford Lectures at Edinburgh were published as *Biblical Faith and Natural Theology*.[^knight-2008][^gifford-barr]

# Work
- *The Semantics of Biblical Language* (1961).[^knight-2008]
- *Fundamentalism* (1977).[^knight-2008]
- *Biblical Faith and Natural Theology: The Gifford Lectures for 1991* (Clarendon Press, 1993).[^knight-2008][^jones-1995]
- *The Concept of Biblical Theology: An Old Testament Perspective* (1999).[^knight-2008]
