# Description
William Lane Craig is a philosopher and Christian apologist, emeritus research professor of philosophy at Talbot School of Theology, Biola University, who from 2014 also taught at Houston Christian University.[^craig-cv][^biola-faculty][^biola-2014][^loc-craig] He argues that God may have ordered the world so that those who never hear the gospel would not have responded to it. On that view no one is lost by historical or geographical accident.[^craig-1989]

# Work
- *The Kalam Cosmological Argument* (1979).[^craig-1979]
- *The Only Wise God: The Compatibility of Divine Foreknowledge and Human Freedom* (1987).[^craig-1987]
- "'No Other Name': A Middle Knowledge Perspective on the Exclusivity of Salvation through Christ", *Faith and Philosophy* 6/2 (1989), 172–188.[^craig-1989]
- "Middle Knowledge and Christian Exclusivism", *Sophia* 34/1 (1995), 120–139.[^craig-1995]

# Stated Position
On his own site he writes that some may reach salvation through general revelation, but "if we take Scripture seriously we must admit that these are relatively few".[^craig-particularism]
