# Description
Francis Watson is a New Testament scholar and professor in the Department of Theology and Religion at Durham University, after posts at Aberdeen and King's College London.[^durham-watson] He writes on Paul, the Gospels and how the four Gospels came to stand together.[^durham-watson]

# Work
- "The Fourfold Gospel", in *The Cambridge Companion to the Gospels*, ed. Stephen C. Barton (Cambridge University Press, 2006), 34–52.[^watson-2006]
- *Gospel Writing: A Canonical Perspective* (Eerdmans, 2013).[^durham-watson]
- *The Fourfold Gospel: A Theological Reading of the New Testament Portraits of Jesus* (Baker Academic, 2016).[^durham-watson]
