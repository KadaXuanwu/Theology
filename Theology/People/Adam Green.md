# Description
Adam Green is a philosopher at the University of Oklahoma who works on divine hiddenness and on social epistemology. He writes the Stanford Encyclopedia entry on hiddenness with Daniel Howard-Snyder.[^ou-green][^sep-hiddenness]

# Work
- *Hidden Divinity and Religious Belief: New Perspectives*, edited with Eleonore Stump (2015).[^ou-green]
- *The Social Contexts of Intellectual Virtue: Knowledge as a Team Achievement* (2016).[^ou-green]
- "Hiddenness of God", with Daniel Howard-Snyder, *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2016, revised 2022).[^sep-hiddenness]
