# Description
Kenneth Kitchen was an Egyptologist at Liverpool from 1951 until his retirement in 1996, and Brunner Professor of Egyptology there. *On the Reliability of the Old Testament* is the fullest case that the Hebrew Bible's historical claims hold up against the ancient near eastern record.[^ees-kitchen][^davey-kitchen]

# Work
- *The Third Intermediate Period in Egypt (1100–650 BC)* (1972).[^davey-kitchen]
- *On the Reliability of the Old Testament* (2003).[^davey-kitchen]
- *Treaty, Law and Covenant in the Ancient Near East* (2012).[^davey-kitchen]

# Stated Position
Writing about his student years and the Christian Union at Liverpool, he described his own conversion as coming through a quiet, carefully thought through trial period to its clear conclusion.[^davey-kitchen]
