# Description
Jason Marsh is a philosopher at St Olaf College who works on divine hiddenness, the cognitive science of religion and bioethics.[^stolaf-marsh] He replied to Maitzen's demographic argument and has since argued that unbelief among prehistoric humans is the harder case for theism than the modern demographic pattern.[^marsh-2008][^marsh-2013]

# Work
- "Do the demographics of theistic belief disconfirm theism? A reply to Maitzen", *Religious Studies* 44/4 (2008), 465–471.[^marsh-2008]
- "Darwin and the Problem of Natural Nonbelief", *The Monist* 96/3 (2013), 349–376.[^marsh-2013]
- "Does God Care About What People Think About God? Revisiting the Problem of Natural Nonbelief", in *The Palgrave Handbook on the Problem of Animal Suffering in the Philosophy of Religion* (2025), 87–107.[^marsh-2025]
