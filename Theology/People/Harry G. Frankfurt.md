# Description
Harry Frankfurt taught philosophy at Rockefeller University, Yale and Princeton, where he was emeritus after 2002.[^wiki-frankfurt][^loc-frankfurt] His work on the will runs from the 1971 paper on freedom of the will to *The Reasons of Love*, and his 1973 essay gives the test on which a threat coerces only when it overbears the will.[^frankfurt-1971][^honderich-1973][^gillham-2020]

# Work
- "Freedom of the Will and the Concept of a Person", *The Journal of Philosophy* 68/1 (1971), beginning p. 5; the end page is not confirmed.[^frankfurt-1971]
- "Coercion and Moral Responsibility", in Ted Honderich (ed.), *Essays on Freedom of Action* (Routledge & Kegan Paul, 1973).[^honderich-1973]
- *The Importance of What We Care About* (Cambridge University Press, 1988).[^openlibrary-frankfurt]
- *The Reasons of Love* (Princeton University Press, 2004).[^openlibrary-frankfurt]
