# Description
William L. Rowe was a philosopher of religion at Purdue University, known for his work on the cosmological argument and the problem of evil.[^draper-2015] He earned his doctorate at the University of Michigan under [[William P. Alston]].[^draper-2015]

# Work
- *The Cosmological Argument* (1975).[^rowe-1975]
- "The Problem of Evil and Some Varieties of Atheism", *American Philosophical Quarterly* 16/4 (1979), 335–341.[^rowe-1979]
- "Religious Experience and the Principle of Credulity", *International Journal for Philosophy of Religion* 13/2 (1982), 85–92.[^rowe-1982]

# Stated Position
In 1979 he wrote: "In this final part of the paper I propose to discuss and defend the position of friendly atheism."[^rowe-1979] A friendly atheist, he explained, "may believe that some theists are rationally justified in believing that the theistic God exists".[^rowe-1979]
