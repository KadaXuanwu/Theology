# Description
James B. Prothro is a New Testament scholar who teaches Scripture and theology at the Augustine Institute Graduate School.[^augustine-prothro][^gnd-prothro] He holds a doctorate in New Testament from Cambridge and a master's degree in classics from Washington University in St. Louis.[^augustine-prothro]

# Work
- *Both Judge and Justifier: Biblical Legal Language and the Act of Justifying in Paul* (Mohr Siebeck, 2018).[^hbz-prothro-judge]
- "Myths about Classical Literature: Responsibly Comparing the New Testament to Ancient Works", in *Myths and Mistakes in New Testament Textual Criticism*, ed. Elijah Hixson and Peter J. Gurry (IVP Academic, 2019).[^loc-myths][^hbz-myths]
- An interview on that chapter with the Center for the Study of New Testament Manuscripts (2020).[^prothro-2020]
