# Description
R. Zachary Manis is a philosopher who taught at Southwest Baptist University from 2005 until 2021 and now directs character formation at Gravitas, an extension of The Stony Brook School.[^manis-scholar][^manis-oup] The vault cites him for a Kierkegaardian account on which the damned hold to hell for the sake of demonstrating that God is in the wrong.[^manis-2016]

# Work
- *Philosophy of Religion: Thinking about Faith*, 2nd edn, with C. Stephen Evans (IVP Academic, 2009).[^manis-oup]
- "'Eternity Will Nail Him to Himself': The Logic of Damnation in Kierkegaard's *The Sickness unto Death*", *Religious Studies* 52/3 (2016), 287–314.[^manis-2016]
- *Sinners in the Presence of a Loving God: An Essay on the Problem of Hell* (Oxford University Press, 2019).[^manis-oup]
- *Thinking Through the Problem of Hell: The Divine Presence Model* (Cascade Books, 2024).[^manis-scholar]

# Stated Position
He defends the divine presence model of hell in his own books rather than stating a position about it separately, and no first-person statement was opened in print.
