# Description
R. Zachary Manis is a philosopher of religion who taught at Southwest Baptist University in Bolivar, Missouri, and works on the problem of hell and on Kierkegaard.[^hendrix][^sbu][^manis-2016] He named what he calls the doxastic problem of hell: that belief in hell "seems incompatible with other beliefs, actions, and attitudes that are fundamental to the faith".[^manis-2015]

# Work
- *Philosophy of Religion: Thinking About Faith*, with [[C. Stephen Evans]], 2nd edn (IVP Academic, 2009).[^evans-manis-2009]
- "The Doxastic Problem of Hell", in *Oxford Studies in Philosophy of Religion*, vol. 6 (2015), 203–223.[^manis-2015]
- "'Eternity will nail him to himself': the logic of damnation in Kierkegaard's *The Sickness unto Death*", *Religious Studies* 52/3 (2016), 287–314.[^manis-2016]
- *Sinners in the Presence of a Loving God: An Essay on the Problem of Hell* (Oxford University Press, 2019), whose chapter 2, "The Doxastic Problem", runs 47–66.[^manis-2019]

# Stated Position
In "The Doxastic Problem of Hell" (2015): "Committed as I am to orthodox Christian theism, however, I assume there must be an adequate solution to all of the problems I have presented."[^manis-2015]
