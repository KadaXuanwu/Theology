# Description
Michael Fishbane is an American scholar of the Bible and its Jewish interpretation who taught at Brandeis University and then served as Nathan Cummings Professor of Jewish Studies at the University of Chicago.[^garber-2007] His *Biblical Interpretation in Ancient Israel* traces [[Inner-biblical exegesis|exegesis within the Bible itself]], scribal, legal, theological and prophetic, and in Zev Garber's summary shows Hebrew Scripture to be "an exegetical work in its own right".[^garber-2007]

# Work
- *Text and Texture: Studies in Biblical Literature* (1979).[^garber-2007]
- *Biblical Interpretation in Ancient Israel* (1985).[^garber-2007]
- *The Garments of Torah: Essays in Biblical Hermeneutics* (1989).[^garber-2007]
- *Biblical Myth and Rabbinic Mythmaking* (2003).[^garber-2007]
