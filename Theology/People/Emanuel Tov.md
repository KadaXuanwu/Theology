# Description
Emanuel Tov is a textual critic of the Hebrew Bible and J. L. Magnes Professor of Bible emeritus at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.[^thetorah-tov] He edited 33 volumes of *Discoveries in the Judaean Desert*.[^thetorah-tov]

# Work
- *Textual Criticism of the Hebrew Bible*, English edition 1992, now in its fourth edition.[^thetorah-tov][^openlibrary-tov]
- "The Essence and History of the Masoretic Text", *Bogoslovlje* 76/1 (2017), 7–26.[^tov-2017]
- "The Socio-Religious Setting of the (Proto-)Masoretic Text", *Textus* 27/1 (2018), 135–153.[^tov-2018]
