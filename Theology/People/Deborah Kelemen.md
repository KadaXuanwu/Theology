# Description
Deborah Kelemen is a psychologist at Boston University and directs its Child Cognition Laboratory. Her work is on how children and adults reason about purpose and design in nature.[^bu-kelemen]

# Work
- "The Scope of Teleological Thinking in Preschool Children", *Cognition* 70 (1999), 241–272.[^bu-kelemen]
- "Are Children 'Intuitive Theists'? Reasoning About Purpose and Design in Nature", *Psychological Science* 15 (2004), 295–301.[^kelemen-2004]
- "Professional Physical Scientists Display Tenacious Teleological Tendencies", with Joshua Rottman and Rebecca Seston, *Journal of Experimental Psychology: General* 142 (2013), 1074–1083.[^kelemen-2013]
