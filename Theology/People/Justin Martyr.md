# Description
Justin Martyr was a Christian apologist who wrote in defence of Christianity and taught at Rome.[^lebreton-1910] He used the Hebrew prophets as evidence for Christ in his Apology and in his Dialogue with the Jew Trypho.[^lebreton-1910]

# Work
- *First Apology*, written between 153 and 155 according to the Catholic Encyclopedia, which dates it from the 150 years since Christ's birth that the text names, from Marcion's spread and from the term of the [[Prefect|prefect]] of Egypt Felix.[^lebreton-1910]
- *Second Apology*, which the same article treats as an appendix to the first.[^lebreton-1910]
- *Dialogue with Trypho*, later than the First Apology and written before 161, the year Antoninus Pius died.[^lebreton-1910]

# Stated Position
In the First Apology he wrote that Christians believe a crucified man is the first-born of God only because "we had found testimonies concerning Him published before He came and was born as man", and because events had followed them.[^justin-apol-53] In the Dialogue he told Trypho that Jewish teachers had cut passages out of the Septuagint, and he proceeded from "those passages which are still admitted by you".[^justin-dial-71]
