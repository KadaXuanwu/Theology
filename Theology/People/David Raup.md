# Description
David Raup was a palaeontologist who worked on extinction and on what the fossil record can and cannot show about it. He taught at Rochester and Chicago and was dean of science at the Field Museum.[^uchicago-raup]

# Work
- *Principles of Paleontology*, with Steven M. Stanley (1971).[^uchicago-raup]
- *The Nemesis Affair: A Story of the Death of the Dinosaurs and the Ways of Science* (1986).[^uchicago-raup]
- *Extinction: Bad Genes or Bad Luck?* (1991).[^uchicago-raup]
