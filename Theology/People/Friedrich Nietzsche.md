# Description
Friedrich Nietzsche was a German philosopher who taught at the University of Basel from 1869 until he resigned in 1879, then wrote with no fixed home until his collapse in Turin in January 1889.[^sep-nietzsche] In *Daybreak* §91 he asks whether a God who let doubt stand for thousands of years, while announcing dreadful consequences for mistaking his truth, can be good.[^nietzsche-daybreak]

# Work
- *The Birth of Tragedy* (1872).[^sep-nietzsche]
- *Daybreak* (1881).[^sep-nietzsche]
- *The Gay Science* (1882, expanded 1887).[^sep-nietzsche]
- *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* (1883–1885).[^sep-nietzsche]
- *On the Genealogy of Morality* (1887).[^sep-nietzsche]
- *The Antichrist* (written 1888, published 1895).[^sep-nietzsche]

# Stated Position
In *The Antichrist* §62 he writes "I condemn Christianity", and calls the Christian church "the greatest of all imaginable corruptions".[^nietzsche-antichrist]
