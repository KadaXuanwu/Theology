# Description
Brian Davies is a Dominican priest and Distinguished Professor of Philosophy at Fordham University, and taught at Oxford before he moved there.[^davies-fordham][^blackfriars-davies][^loc-davies] He holds that God is not a moral agent subject to duties and obligations.[^sassi-2008]

# Work
- *An Introduction to the Philosophy of Religion* (Oxford University Press, 1982).[^davies-fordham]
- *The Reality of God and the Problem of Evil* (Continuum, 2006).[^davies-fordham]
- *Thomas Aquinas on God and Evil* (Oxford University Press, 2011).[^davies-fordham]

# Stated Position
In a Fordham report on *The Reality of God and the Problem of Evil* he is quoted: "I want to reject the theistic and nontheistic discussions of God and evil that start from the presupposition that God is the sort of thing I am, a moral agent subject to duties and obligations."[^sassi-2008]
