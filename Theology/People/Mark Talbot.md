# Description
Mark Talbot is a philosopher, emeritus at Wheaton College, who writes on suffering and on the philosophical implications of Reformed theology.[^wheaton-talbot][^loc-talbot]

# Work
- "'All the Good that is Ours in Christ'", in *Suffering and the Sovereignty of God* (2006).[^wheaton-talbot]
- *When the Stars Disappear* (2020).[^crossway-talbot]
- *Give Me Understanding That I May Live* (2022).[^crossway-talbot]

# Stated Position
None found in print. He describes his own research as defending the philosophical implications of Reformed and post-Reformation theology, including everlasting punishment, which is a statement about what he defends rather than about what he believes.[^wheaton-talbot]
