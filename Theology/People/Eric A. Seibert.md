# Description
Eric A. Seibert is an Old Testament scholar who taught at Messiah University for 28 years, until 2024.[^messiah-2024] His research deals with the troubling portrayals of God in the Old Testament and with reading the Bible in ways that promote peace.[^wjk-seibert]

# Work
- *Disturbing Divine Behavior: Troubling Old Testament Images of God* (Fortress Press, 2009).[^seibert-2009]
- *The Violence of Scripture: Overcoming the Old Testament's Troubling Legacy* (Fortress Press, 2012).[^seibert-2012]
- *Redeeming Violent Verses: A Guide for Using Troublesome Texts in Church and Ministry* (Westminster John Knox, 2023).[^wjk-seibert]

# Stated Position
On the order to destroy Amalek in [1 Samuel 15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Samuel%2015&version=NIV), he writes that "God never issued this genocidal decree".[^seibert-2009]
