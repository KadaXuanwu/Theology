# Description
J. L. Schellenberg is a philosopher at Mount Saint Vincent University in Halifax. The hiddenness argument is his, and so is the term non-resistant non-belief that the vault's material on honest unbelief turns on.[^schellenberg-site]

# Work
- *Divine Hiddenness and Human Reason* (1993).[^schellenberg-site]
- *The Hiddenness Argument: Philosophy's New Challenge to Belief in God* (2015).[^schellenberg-site]
- *What God Would Have Known: How Human Intellectual and Moral Development Undermines Christian Doctrine* (2024).[^schellenberg-site]

# Stated Position
He writes that he is not a Christian and last held a Christian commitment in early adulthood.[^schellenberg-oup] He separates his atheism about a personal God from what he calls sceptical religion, a religious stance that does not rest on belief, so atheist alone misdescribes him.[^schellenberg-site]
