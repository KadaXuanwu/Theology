# Description
John Barton is an Old Testament scholar, emeritus Oriel and Laing Professor of the Interpretation of Holy Scripture at Oxford and a Senior Research Fellow of Campion Hall.[^oxford-barton] His research covers the biblical [[Canon|canon]], biblical ethics, the prophets and the history of scholarship.[^oxford-barton]

# Work
- *Oracles of God: Perceptions of Ancient Prophecy in Israel after the Exile* (Darton, Longman & Todd, 1986).[^barton-pubs]
- *The Spirit and the Letter: Studies in the Biblical Canon* (SPCK, 1997), published in the United States as *Holy Writings, Sacred Text* (Westminster John Knox, 1998).[^barton-pubs]
- *A History of the Bible: The Book and Its Faiths* (Allen Lane, 2019).[^barton-pubs]

# Stated Position
In a 2019 *Church Times* interview, as quoted on the blog *Psephizo*, he said: "I believe the Bible contains and imparts a great deal of wisdom, but I tend not to think of it as 'inspired', preferring the idea that 'the Bible tells us what we cannot tell ourselves', as Lutherans tend to put it".[^paul-2019]
