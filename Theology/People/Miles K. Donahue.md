# Description
Miles K. Donahue is a philosopher at the University of Oxford who writes on methodological naturalism and on the fine tuning argument for God.[^donahue-2025][^donahue-2026]

# Work
- "Methodological Naturalism, Analyzed", *Erkenntnis* 90/5 (2025), 1981–2002. It argues "in favor of a restricted version" of methodological naturalism.[^donahue-2025]
- "Divine Psychology and Cosmic Fine-Tuning", *Religious Studies* 61/4 (2025), 794–810.[^donahue-2025-psychology]
- "Sceptical Theism Undermines the Fine-Tuning Argument. Mostly", *Religious Studies* 62/1 (2026), 164–183.[^donahue-2026]
