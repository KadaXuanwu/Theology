# Description
Sharon Zuckerman was an archaeologist at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, a senior lecturer who headed its section for the archaeology of the biblical period.[^young-academy][^bar-zuckerman] She supervised an area at Hazor and later co-directed the excavations there with [[Amnon Ben-Tor]].[^young-academy][^bhd-zuckerman]

# Work
- "Where Is the Hazor Archive Buried?" (2006).[^zuckerman-2006]
- "Anatomy of a Destruction: Crisis Architecture, Termination Rituals and the Fall of Canaanite Hazor" (2007).[^young-academy]

# Stated Position
On who destroyed Canaanite Hazor: "more recent dating of this destruction places it too early for the Israelites. ... I believe it was an internal revolt within the city that was responsible for the destruction."[^zuckerman-2006]
