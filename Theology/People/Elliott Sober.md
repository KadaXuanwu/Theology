# Description
Elliott Sober is a philosopher, retired from the Department of Philosophy at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.[^uw-sober] He has written on design arguments and on the philosophy of evolutionary theory.[^sober-2002][^sober-2018][^sober-site]

# Work
- "Intelligent Design and Probability Reasoning", *International Journal for Philosophy of Religion* 52/2 (2002), 65–80.[^sober-2002]
- "The Design Argument", in *The Blackwell Guide to the Philosophy of Religion*, ed. William E. Mann (2005), 117–147.[^sober-2005]
- *The Design Argument* (Cambridge University Press, 2018).[^sober-2018]
