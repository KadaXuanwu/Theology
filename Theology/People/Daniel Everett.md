# Description
Daniel Everett is a linguist at Bentley University who spent decades with the Pirahã of the Amazon. He went as a missionary and left without his religion, and his claim that the language has no recursion is contested.[^everett-about]

# Work
- *Don't Sleep, There Are Snakes: Life and Language in the Amazonian Jungle* (2008).[^everett-about]
- *Language: The Cultural Tool* (2012).[^everett-about]
- *How Language Began* (2017).[^everett-about]

# Stated Position
He writes on his own site that his interest in linguistics, anthropology and philosophy grew over the years in the field, and that the Pirahã were a major influence on his decision to abandon missions and religious belief.[^everett-about]
