# Description
Richard B. Hays was a New Testament scholar, George Washington Ivey Professor of New Testament and former dean of Duke Divinity School.[^duke-hays][^ct-hays] He was known for his work on the Gospels, the letters of Paul and New Testament ethics.[^duke-hays]

# Work
- *Echoes of Scripture in the Letters of Paul* (1989).[^openlibrary-hays]
- *The Moral Vision of the New Testament* (1996).[^duke-hays][^openlibrary-hays]
- *Reading Backwards: Figural Christology and the Fourfold Gospel Witness* (2014).[^openlibrary-hays]
- *Echoes of Scripture in the Gospels* (2016).[^openlibrary-hays]
- *The Widening of God's Mercy: Sexuality Within the Biblical Story* (2024), with Christopher B. Hays.[^duke-hays][^openlibrary-hays]

# Stated Position
In a 2016 interview he said: "That's why I believe that the Old Testament and the New have an underlying narrative unity that can be discerned only in retrospect, when we read the whole thing together."[^hays-2016]
