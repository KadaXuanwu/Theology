# Description
Jeff Jordan was a philosopher of religion at the University of Delaware, whose work centred on pragmatic reasons for belief and on the problem of evil.[^udel-2025][^loc-jordan] He wrote the *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* entry on pragmatic arguments for belief in God.[^jordan-sep]

# Work
- *Gambling on God: Essays on Pascal's Wager*, editor (Rowman & Littlefield, 1994).[^udel-2025][^dailynous-2025]
- *Pascal's Wager: Pragmatic Arguments and Belief in God* (Clarendon Press, 2006).[^jordan-sep]
- "The Wager and William James", in *Pascal's Wager*, ed. Paul Bartha and Lawrence Pasternack (Cambridge University Press, 2018), 101–120.[^jordan-2018]
- "Pragmatic Arguments and Belief in God", *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (first published 2004, revised 2022).[^jordan-sep]
