# Description
Ulrich Berges is a German Catholic scholar of the Old Testament who held chairs of Old Testament exegesis in Lima, Nijmegen, Münster and Bonn.[^gnd-berges] His books include a study of the composition of the book of Isaiah and a commentary on Isaiah 40–54.[^lobid-1998][^lobid-2008][^lobid-2015]

# Work
- *Das Buch Jesaja: Komposition und Endgestalt* (Herder, 1998).[^lobid-1998]
- *Jesaja 40–48*, Herders theologischer Kommentar zum Alten Testament 36 (Herder, 2008).[^lobid-2008]
- *Isaiah: The Prophet and His Book*, trans. Philip Sumpter (Sheffield Phoenix Press, 2012).[^berges-2012-book][^lobid-2012]
- "The Fourth Servant Song (Isaiah 52:13–53:12)" (2012).[^berges-2012-ote] It says at p. 491 that Isaiah 53 "actually refers to the group of returnees or rather a part thereof."[^berges-2012-ote]
- *Jesaja 49–54*, Herders theologischer Kommentar zum Alten Testament 37 (Herder, 2015).[^lobid-2015]
