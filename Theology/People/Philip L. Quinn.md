# Description
Philip L. Quinn was a philosopher of religion who taught at Brown University and then at the University of Notre Dame, where he held the John A. O'Brien chair of philosophy.[^nd-2004][^miller-2006] He wrote on divine commands and morality and on the epistemology of religious belief, and argued with [[Alvin Plantinga]] over whether belief in God can be [[Properly basic belief|properly basic]] for intellectually sophisticated adults.[^miller-2006]

# Work
- *Divine Commands and Moral Requirements* (Clarendon Press, 1978).[^miller-2006]
- "In Search of the Foundations of Theism", *Faith and Philosophy* 2/4 (1985), 469–486.[^quinn-1985]
- *Essays in the Philosophy of Religion*, ed. Christian B. Miller (Clarendon Press, 2006), published after his death.[^miller-2006]
