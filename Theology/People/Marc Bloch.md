# Description
Marc Bloch was a French historian who co-founded the *Annales* with Lucien Febvre, joined the resistance and was shot by the Germans in 1944. *Les Rois thaumaturges* is the foundational study of the royal touch.[^britannica-bloch][^cercleshoah-bloch]

# Work
- *Les Rois thaumaturges* (1924), in English *The Royal Touch*.[^bloch-1924]
- *Les Caractères originaux de l'histoire rurale française* (1931).[^britannica-bloch]
- *Apologie pour l'histoire*, published after his death (1949).[^britannica-bloch]

# Stated Position
In his testament, written at Clermont-Ferrand in March 1941, he asks for no Hebrew prayers at his grave, saying he recognises no orthodoxy's creed and is a stranger to confessional formalism, while affirming that he was born a Jew.[^bloch-testament] That is about Jewish religious practice, not about Christian truth claims.
