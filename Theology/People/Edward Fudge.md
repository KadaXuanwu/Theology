# Description
Edward Fudge was a theologian and lawyer who made the modern case for conditional immortality, the view that the lost are destroyed rather than kept alive in torment. He preached in Churches of Christ before practising law in Houston.[^chronicle-fudge][^loc-fudge]

# Work
- *The Fire That Consumes: A Biblical and Historical Study of the Doctrine of Final Punishment* (1982, third edition 2011).[^loc-fudge]
- *Two Views of Hell: A Biblical and Theological Dialogue*, with Robert A. Peterson (2000).[^loc-fudge]
- *Hell: A Final Word* (2012).[^loc-fudge]

# Stated Position
In *The Fire That Consumes* he describes himself as a theist, a Christian and an evangelical who takes Scripture to be the word of God written, without error in anything it teaches. Read at second hand through a review that gives the page, so the page is unconfirmed.
