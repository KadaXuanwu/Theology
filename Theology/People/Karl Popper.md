# Description
Karl Popper was a philosopher of science who spent the war years in New Zealand and then held the chair in logic and scientific method at the London School of Economics from 1949 to 1969. Falsifiability is his, and so is most of what the vault says about what makes a claim testable.[^britannica-popper][^sep-popper]

# Work
- *Logik der Forschung* (1934), in English *The Logic of Scientific Discovery* (1959).[^sep-popper]
- *The Open Society and Its Enemies* (1945).[^britannica-popper]
- *Conjectures and Refutations* (1963).[^britannica-popper]

# Stated Position
In an interview given in 1969 on condition it stay unpublished in his lifetime, he said he did not know whether God exists and called agnosticism, admitting we do not know and going on searching, the right position.[^zerin-1998]
