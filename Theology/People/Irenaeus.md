# Description
Irenaeus was a presbyter and then bishop of the church at Lyon in Gaul, and as a boy in Asia he had heard Polycarp, bishop of Smyrna.[^eusebius-he5] He wrote *Against Heresies* against the Gnostic teachers of his day, and in it argues that the church has four Gospels, no more and no fewer.[^graz-irenaeus][^irenaeus-ah]

# Work
- *Against Heresies*, in five books, written while Eleutherus was bishop of Rome, about 180.[^eusebius-he5][^graz-irenaeus]
- *Demonstration of the Apostolic Preaching*, which Eusebius says he dedicated to a brother named Marcian.[^eusebius-he5][^graz-irenaeus]
- *On Monarchy, or That God Is Not the Author of Evil*, a letter to Florinus, part of which Eusebius preserves.[^eusebius-he5]

# Stated Position
On the number of the Gospels, in *Against Heresies* 3.11.8: "It is not possible that the Gospels can be either more or fewer in number than they are." In the same passage he says the Word "has given us the Gospel under four aspects, but bound together by one Spirit".[^irenaeus-ah]
