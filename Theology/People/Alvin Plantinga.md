# Description
Alvin Plantinga is a philosopher, emeritus at Notre Dame, who taught at Calvin from 1963 to 1982. His reformed epistemology is the standard case that belief in God can be rational without an argument behind it, and it is what most of the vault's warrant material answers.[^nd-plantinga][^templeton-plantinga]

# Work
- *Warrant and Proper Function* (1993).[^nd-plantinga]
- *Warranted Christian Belief* (2000).[^nd-plantinga]
- *Where the Conflict Really Lies: Science, Religion and Naturalism* (2011).[^nd-plantinga]

# Stated Position
In "Advice to Christian Philosophers", his 1983 inaugural lecture at Notre Dame, he writes as a Christian addressing Christian philosophers and argues they should not settle for being philosophers who happen to be Christians.[^plantinga-advice]
