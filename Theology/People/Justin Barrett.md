# Description
Justin Barrett is a psychologist working in the cognitive science of religion, founder of Blueprint 1543 and an honorary professor at St Andrews. He was at Oxford's Institute of Cognitive and Evolutionary Anthropology and then Fuller Theological Seminary.[^standrews-barrett][^loc-barrett]

# Work
- *Why Would Anyone Believe in God?* (2004).[^loc-barrett]
- *Cognitive Science, Religion, and Theology* (2011).[^loc-barrett]
- *Born Believers: The Science of Children's Religious Belief* (2012).[^loc-barrett]

# Stated Position
Under his own byline he writes that he is convinced Christian faith is not only compatible with a career in the sciences but a strong foundation for one.[^barrett-blueprint]
