# Description
John Calvin was a French reformer who led the church in Geneva from 1541 until his death, with an earlier spell there and three years in Strasbourg. The *Institutes* and his commentary on Romans are where the vault's material on general revelation and the sense of the divine comes from.[^britannica-calvin][^loc-calvin]

# Work
- *Institutes of the Christian Religion* (1536, final Latin edition 1559).[^britannica-calvin]
- *Commentary on Romans*, its dedication dated Strasbourg, 18 October 1539.[^calvin-romans]
- *Commentary on the Psalms*, with his own account of his conversion in the preface.[^calvin-psalms]

# Stated Position
In the preface to the Psalms commentary he writes that God by a sudden conversion subdued his mind and brought it to a teachable frame, and tells the story of his break with what he calls the superstitions of the papacy.[^calvin-psalms]
