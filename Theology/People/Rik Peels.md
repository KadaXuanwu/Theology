# Description
Rik Peels is a philosopher at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam working on the ethics of belief, ignorance and scientism.[^vu-peels] He is the vault's source for an account of responsible belief that holds even though belief is not directly chosen.[^peels-2017]

# Work
- *Responsible Belief: A Theory in Ethics and Epistemology* (Oxford University Press, 2017).[^peels-2017]
- *The Epistemic Dimensions of Ignorance*, co-edited (2016).[^vu-peels]
- *Scientism: A Philosophical Exposition and Evaluation*, co-edited (Oxford University Press, 2017).[^vu-peels]
- "Responsible Belief, Influence, and Control: Response to Stephen White", *Journal of Philosophical Research* 44 (2019), 53–62.[^peels-2019]

# Stated Position
None found in print. His published work is argument rather than testimony, and no statement of his own stance on religion appeared on any professionally published page checked.
