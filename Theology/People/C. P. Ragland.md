# Description
C. P. Ragland is a philosopher at Saint Louis University, where he chairs the department. Most of his work is on Descartes, and a second strand is on hell and divine justice.[^slu-ragland]

# Work
- "Hell", *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2008).[^iep-hell]
- "Love and Damnation", in *Metaphysics and God: Essays in Honor of Eleonore Stump* (2009).[^slu-ragland]
- *The Will to Reason: Theodicy and Freedom in Descartes* (2016).[^slu-ragland]

None found in print.
