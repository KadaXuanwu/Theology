# Description
Stephen T. Davis is a philosopher of religion and emeritus Russell K. Pitzer Professor of Philosophy at Claremont McKenna College.[^cmc-davis] His books and articles treat the resurrection and the fate of those who never hear the gospel.[^cmc-davis]

# Work
- "Universalism, Hell, and the Fate of the Ignorant", *Modern Theology* 6/2 (1990), 173–186.[^davis-1990]
- *Risen Indeed: Making Sense of the Resurrection* (1993).[^cmc-davis]
- *Christian Philosophical Theology* (2006).[^cmc-davis]
- *Rational Faith: A Philosopher's Defense of Christianity* (2016).[^cmc-davis]

# Stated Position
"As an orthodox Christian, then, I do believe that salvation is to be found only in Christ." He adds, "I am not a universalist myself."[^davis-1990]
