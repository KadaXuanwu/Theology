# Description
Rudolf Bultmann was a German New Testament scholar, professor at Marburg from 1921 to 1951.[^nce-bultmann] His 1941 lecture "Neues Testament und Mythologie" argued that the New Testament "must be stripped of its mythological trappings and restated in language that addresses man in his existential condition".[^nce-bultmann]

# Work
- *Die Geschichte der synoptischen Tradition* (1921).[^nce-bultmann]
- *Das Evangelium des Johannes* (Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1941); in English as *The Gospel of John: A Commentary* (Blackwell, 1971).[^nce-bultmann]
- "Neues Testament und Mythologie" (1941); in English in *Kerygma and Myth* (SPCK, 1953).[^nce-bultmann]
- *Theologie des Neuen Testaments*, two volumes (1948–1953).[^nce-bultmann]

# Stated Position
Replying to his critics, he wrote that the Bible "assumes the shape of a word which addresses me personally", and that "in traditional language, that is the work of the Holy Ghost".[^bultmann-kerygma]
