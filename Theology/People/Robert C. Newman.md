# Description
Robert C. Newman is professor emeritus of New Testament at Biblical Theological Seminary and director of the Interdisciplinary Biblical Research Institute, with a doctorate in theoretical astrophysics from Cornell University.[^biblical-elearning] He is a past president of the Evangelical Theological Society and has written on fulfilled prophecy and on science and the Bible.[^biblical-elearning]

# Work
- *The Evidence of Prophecy*, editor (Interdisciplinary Biblical Research Institute, 1988).[^biblical-elearning]
- "Fulfilled prophecy as miracle", paper at the Evangelical Theological Society, 1996.[^asbury-1996]
- Robert C. Newman, John A. Bloom and Hugh G. Gauch Jr., "Public Theology and Prophecy Data: Factual Evidence That Counts for the Biblical World View", *JETS* 46/1 (2003), 79–110.[^newman-2003]
- Hugh G. Gauch Jr., Robert C. Newman and John A. Bloom, "Prophecy Arguments in Apostolic and Contemporary Times", *JETS* 65/1 (2022), 37–46.[^gauch-2022]

# Stated Position
In a 2003 article written with John A. Bloom and Hugh G. Gauch Jr. he wrote: "The key feature of prophecy which allows it to provide humans with evidence that God exists is that successful predictions, though hard to make, can be easy to check."[^newman-2003]
