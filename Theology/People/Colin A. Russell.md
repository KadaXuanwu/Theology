# Description
Colin A. Russell was a historian of science who founded and led the Open University's department of history of science and technology. His chapter in Ferngren's *Science and Religion* is the standard short statement of why the conflict thesis fails as history.[^faraday-russell][^ou-russell]

# Work
- *Cross-Currents: Interactions between Science and Faith* (1985).[^faraday-russell]
- *Michael Faraday: Physics and Faith* (2000).[^faraday-russell]
- "The Conflict of Science and Religion", in Gary B. Ferngren (ed.), *Science and Religion: A Historical Introduction* (2002).[^russell-ferngren]

# Stated Position
None found in print. He led Christians in Science and helped found the International Society for Science and Religion, which is affiliation rather than a statement.[^faraday-russell]
