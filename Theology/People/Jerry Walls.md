# Description
Jerry Walls is a philosopher who taught at Asbury Theological Seminary from 1987 and then at Houston Christian University until his retirement in 2025. His trilogy on hell, heaven and purgatory is the standard analytic treatment of the free choice reading of damnation.[^wiki-walls][^loc-walls]

# Work
- *Hell: The Logic of Damnation* (1992).[^loc-walls]
- *Heaven: The Logic of Eternal Joy* (2002).[^loc-walls]
- *Purgatory: The Logic of Total Transformation* (2011).[^loc-walls]

# Stated Position
None found in print. His titles state positions inside Christianity, such as *Why I Am Not a Calvinist*, rather than about its truth.
