# Description
Nikk Effingham is Professor of Philosophy and head of department at the University of Birmingham, working mainly on metaphysics: time, time travel, persistence and mereology.[^birmingham-effingham] The vault cites him for a current defence of the Anselmian answer to the proportionality problem of hell, which is a secondary strand in his work rather than his main field.[^effingham-2024]

# Work
- *Does Tomorrow Exist? A Debate*, with Kristie Miller (Routledge, 2023).[^birmingham-effingham]
- "Would God Really Send Me to Hell for Stealing a Wispa Bar?", *Sophia* 63 (2024), 85–97.[^effingham-2024]
- "Simulations All the Way Up! An Atheist's Response to the Fine-Tuning Argument", *Analytic Philosophy* (2026).[^birmingham-effingham]

# Stated Position
None found in print. The title of the 2026 paper names an atheist's response as the position it defends, which is a claim about the argument rather than a statement of his own belief.
