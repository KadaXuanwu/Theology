# Description
Nikk Effingham is Professor of Philosophy and head of department at the University of Birmingham. He lists his research as metaphysics, philosophy of time (mainly time travel), philosophy of religion, and philosophy of epidemiology.[^birmingham-effingham] The vault cites him for the argument that the divine injury response to the problem of hell answers its logical form but not its evidential one.[^effingham-2024]

# Work
- *Does Tomorrow Exist? A Debate*, with Kristie Miller (Routledge, 2023).[^birmingham-effingham]
- "Would God Really Send Me to Hell for Stealing a Wispa Bar?", *Sophia* 63/1 (2024), 85–97.[^effingham-2024]
- "Simulations All the Way Up! An Atheist's Response to the Fine-Tuning Argument", *Analytic Philosophy* (2026).[^birmingham-effingham]
