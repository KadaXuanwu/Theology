# Description
Henry Drummond was a Scottish evangelist and writer who lectured on natural science at the Free Church College in Glasgow and worked for two years in the Moody and Sankey missions. *The Ascent of Man* is where he attacks the habit of hunting for gaps in nature to put God into.[^eb1911-drummond][^smith-drummond]

# Work
- *Natural Law in the Spiritual World* (1883).[^eb1911-drummond]
- *The Greatest Thing in the World* (1890).[^eb1911-drummond]
- *The Ascent of Man* (1894), delivered as the Lowell Lectures in Boston in 1893.[^eb1911-drummond]

# Stated Position
In the preface to *Natural Law in the Spiritual World* he writes that nature is truth as it came from God, to be read with the same open eye and the same reverence as any other revelation.[^drummond-natural-law]
