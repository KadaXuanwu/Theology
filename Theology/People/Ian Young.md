# Description
Ian Young is a scholar of Classical Hebrew and biblical studies and an Honorary Associate Professor in the School of Languages and Cultures at the University of Sydney.[^sydney-young] He took his BA and PhD there.[^sydney-young]

# Work
- "The Stabilization of the Biblical Text in the Light of Qumran and Masada: A Challenge for Conventional Qumran Chronology?", *Dead Sea Discoveries* 9/3 (2002), 364–390.[^young-2002] In a 2004 list post he wrote that he thinks the biblical text "was stabilized" before Masada, and that "The process of stabilization was finalized between the mid-first century BCE (Qumran) and the late first century CE (Masada)".[^young-2004]
