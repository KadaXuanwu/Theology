# Description
Matthew Flannagan is a New Zealand philosopher and theologian who works on divine command ethics and on the conquest texts. He wrote *Did God Really Command Genocide?* with Paul Copan.[^mandm-flannagan][^orcid-flannagan]

# Work
- "Is Ethical Naturalism More Plausible than Supernaturalism? A Reply to Walter Sinnott-Armstrong", *Philo* 15/1 (2012), 19–37.[^mandm-flannagan]
- *Did God Really Command Genocide? Coming to Terms with the Justice of God*, with Paul Copan (2014).[^mandm-flannagan]
- "Robust Ethics and the Autonomy Thesis: A Reply to Erik Wielenberg", *Philosophia Christi* 19/2 (2017), 345–362.[^mandm-flannagan]
