# Description
C. E. B. Cranfield was a New Testament scholar at the University of Durham and the New Testament editor of the International Critical Commentary. His commentary on Romans in that series runs to two volumes.[^dunn-2016]

# Work
- *The First Epistle of Peter* (SCM Press, 1950).[^dunn-2016][^manson-1952]
- *The Gospel According to St Mark*, Cambridge Greek Testament Commentary (1959).[^dunn-2016]
- *A Critical and Exegetical Commentary on the Epistle to the Romans*, International Critical Commentary, volume 1 (1975).[^dunn-2016]
- *Romans: A Shorter Commentary* (1985).[^dunn-2016]
