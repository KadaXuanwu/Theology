# Description
Albert C. Sundberg Jr. was a biblical scholar at Garrett Theological Seminary who wrote on the Old Testament of the early church and the history of the New Testament [[Canon|canon]].[^sundberg-1973][^kruger-2012] [[Michael J. Kruger]] credits him with first defining canon as "a fixed, final, and closed list of books", and with sharply dividing scripture from canon, as others had before him.[^kruger-2012]

# Work
- *The Old Testament of the Early Church*, Harvard Theological Studies 20 (1964).[^sundberg-1973]
- "Towards a Revised History of the New Testament Canon", *Studia Evangelica* 4 (1968), 452–461.[^kruger-2012]
- "Canon Muratori: A Fourth-Century List", *Harvard Theological Review* 66/1 (1973), 1–41.[^sundberg-1973]
- "The Bible Canon and the Christian Doctrine of Inspiration", *Interpretation* 29/4 (1975), 352–371.[^sundberg-1975]

# Stated Position
His 1975 article "The Bible Canon and the Christian Doctrine of Inspiration" takes up how canon and inspiration relate. Its text could not be opened, so no statement of his is quoted here.[^sundberg-1975]
