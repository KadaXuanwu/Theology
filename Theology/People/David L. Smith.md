# Description
David L. Smith is a historian of seventeenth century Britain, a fellow of Selwyn College, Cambridge, since 1988. He works on parliaments, the constitution and the civil wars.[^selwyn-smith][^ca-smith]

# Work
- *Constitutional Royalism and the Search for Settlement, c. 1640–1649* (1994).[^ca-smith]
- *The Stuart Parliaments, 1603–1689* (1999).[^ca-smith]
- "The King's Evil without the King: The Royal Touch during the Interregnum", *Journal of Religious History* 49/4 (2025), 439–454.[^smith-2025]
