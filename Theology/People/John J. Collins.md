# Description
John J. Collins is a scholar of the Hebrew Bible and Holmes Professor Emeritus of Old Testament Criticism and Interpretation at Yale Divinity School.[^yds-collins][^yale-experts] He has written on apocalypticism, wisdom literature, Hellenistic Judaism and the Dead Sea Scrolls.[^yds-collins]

# Work
- *Daniel* (Hermeneia; Fortress, 1993).[^yds-collins]
- *The Scepter and the Star: The Messiahs of the Dead Sea Scrolls and Other Ancient Literature* (Doubleday, 1995); second edition *The Scepter and the Star: Messianism in Light of the Dead Sea Scrolls* (Eerdmans, 2010).[^yds-collins][^eerdmans-scepter]
- *The Apocalyptic Imagination: An Introduction to Jewish Apocalyptic Literature*, third edition (Eerdmans, 2016).[^eerdmans-apoc]
- *A Short Introduction to the Hebrew Bible*, third edition (Fortress Press, 2018).[^fortress-short]
- "Gabriel and David: Some Reflections on an Enigmatic Text", in *Hazon Gabriel: New Readings of the Gabriel Revelation*, ed. Matthias Henze (Society of Biblical Literature, 2011), 99–112.[^sbl-hazon]
