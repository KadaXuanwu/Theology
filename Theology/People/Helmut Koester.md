# Description
Helmut Koester was a New Testament scholar who taught at Harvard Divinity School, where he was John H. Morison Professor of New Testament Studies and Winn Professor of Ecclesiastical History.[^gazette-koester][^gnd-koester] He studied under Rudolf Bultmann at Marburg and taught at Heidelberg before he came to Harvard.[^gazette-koester]

# Work
- *Introduction to the New Testament*, 2 vols (Fortress Press, 1982).[^hbz-koester-introduction]
- "The Text of the Synoptic Gospels in the Second Century" (1989).[^koester-1989]
- *Ancient Christian Gospels: Their History and Development* (Trinity Press International and SCM Press, 1990).[^hbz-koester-gospels]

# Stated Position
No published statement of his own position on the truth of Christianity was found.
