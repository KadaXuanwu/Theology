# Description
James Ware is a New Testament scholar, emeritus at the University of Evansville, who works on Paul and on the resurrection texts.[^evansville-ware][^loc-ware]

# Work
- *The Mission of the Church in Paul's Letter to the Philippians in the Context of Ancient Judaism* (2005).[^loc-ware]
- *Paul's Theology in Context: Creation, Incarnation, Covenant, and Kingdom* (2019).[^loc-ware]
- *The Final Triumph of God: Jesus, the Eyewitnesses, and the Resurrection of the Body in 1 Corinthians 15* (2025).[^loc-ware]
