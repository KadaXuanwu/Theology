# Description
Alan Hájek is a professor of philosophy at the Australian National University, and taught before that at the California Institute of Technology.[^anu-hajek] His paper "What Conditional Probability Could Not Be" won the American Philosophical Association's article prize, and he wrote the *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* entry on Pascal's wager.[^anu-hajek][^hajek-sep]

# Work
- "Objecting Vaguely to Pascal's Wager", *Philosophical Studies* 98/1 (2000), 1–14.[^hajek-2000]
- "What Conditional Probability Could Not Be", *Synthese* 137/3 (2003), 273–323.[^hajek-2003b]
- "Waging War on Pascal's Wager", *Philosophical Review* 112/1 (2003), 27–56.[^hajek-2003]
- "Pascal's Wager", *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (first published 1998, revised 2022).[^hajek-sep]
