# Description
Stephen Maitzen is a philosopher at Acadia University who works on divine hiddenness, the problem of evil and sceptical theism.[^acadia-maitzen] His demographic argument presses the uneven geographic distribution of theistic belief against theistic explanations of individual unbelief.[^maitzen-2006]

# Work
- "Anselmian Atheism" (2005).[^acadia-cv]
- "Divine hiddenness and the demographics of theism", *Religious Studies* 42/2 (2006), 177–191.[^maitzen-2006]
- "Substantial Change: Continuous, Consistent, Objective" (2017).[^acadia-cv]
- *Determinism, Death, and Meaning* (2022).[^acadia-cv]

# Stated Position
He opens an article on purpose with the line "When believers find out I'm an atheist".[^maitzen-2011] It is a movement magazine rather than a peer reviewed journal, which is where the statement about himself sits.
