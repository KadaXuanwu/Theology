# Description
Greg Bahnsen was a Reformed apologist and minister in the Orthodox Presbyterian Church who taught apologetics and ethics at Reformed Theological Seminary. He is the best known defender of the presuppositional argument, that Christianity has to be true for anything to be provable at all.[^opc-bahnsen]

# Work
- *Theonomy in Christian Ethics* (1977).[^opc-bahnsen]
- *Always Ready: Directions for Defending the Faith* (1996).[^bahnsen-always-ready]
- *Van Til's Apologetic: Readings and Analysis* (1998).[^opc-bahnsen]

# Stated Position
His own summary is that the proof Christianity is true is that if it were not, nothing could be proved at all.[^bahnsen-always-ready]
