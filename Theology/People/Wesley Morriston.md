# Description
Wesley Morriston is a philosopher of religion, emeritus at the University of Colorado Boulder. He writes on divine command ethics, on the kalam cosmological argument and on the conquest texts.[^colorado-morriston]

# Work
- "Did God Command Genocide? A Challenge to the Biblical Inerrantist", *Philosophia Christi* 11/1 (2009), 7–26.[^morriston-page]
- "Ethical Criticism of the Bible: The Case of Divinely Mandated Genocide", *Sophia* 51 (2012), 117–135.[^morriston-page]
- "Endless and Infinite", with Alex Malpass, *The Philosophical Quarterly* 70 (2020), 830–849.[^morriston-page]
