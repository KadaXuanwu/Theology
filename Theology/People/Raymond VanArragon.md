# Description
Raymond VanArragon is a philosopher at Bethel University in Minnesota, where he is also dean of faculty. He works on hell, on religious diversity and on whether someone could freely reject God for ever.[^bethel-vanarragon]

# Work
- *Contemporary Debates in Philosophy of Religion*, edited with Michael L. Peterson (2004).[^bethel-vanarragon]
- "Is it Possible to Freely Reject God Forever?", in Joel Buenting (ed.), *The Problem of Hell* (2010), 29–43.[^sep-heaven-hell]
- *Evidence and Religious Belief*, edited with Kelly James Clark (2011).[^bethel-vanarragon]
