# Description
Lawrence H. Schiffman is a specialist in the Dead Sea Scrolls, Judaism in late antiquity and the history of Jewish law, and Global Distinguished Professor of Hebrew and Judaic Studies at New York University, where from 2014 he held the Judge Abraham Lieberman chair.[^aajr-schiffman][^tim-schiffman] He was vice provost for undergraduate education at Yeshiva University and has served as president of the Association for Jewish Studies.[^aajr-schiffman][^ktav-schiffman]

# Work
- *The Halakhah at Qumran* (Brill, 1975).[^schiffman-1975]
- *Who Was a Jew? Rabbinic and Halakhic Perspectives on the Jewish Christian Schism* (Ktav, 1985).[^schiffman-1985]
- *Reclaiming the Dead Sea Scrolls* (Jewish Publication Society, 1994).[^cojs-schiffman]
- *Encyclopedia of the Dead Sea Scrolls* (Oxford University Press, 2000), edited with James C. VanderKam.[^schiffman-2000]
- *Qumran and Jerusalem: Studies in the Dead Sea Scrolls and the History of Judaism* (2010).[^aajr-schiffman]
