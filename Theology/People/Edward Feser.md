# Description
Edward Feser is a philosopher at Pasadena City College who argues for classical theism in the Aristotelian and Thomist line, against both the new atheism and much of contemporary theistic personalism.[^feser-about][^gnd-feser]

# Work
- *The Last Superstition: A Refutation of the New Atheism* (2008).[^feser-about]
- *Scholastic Metaphysics: A Contemporary Introduction* (2014).[^feser-about]
- *Five Proofs of the Existence of God* (2017).[^feser-about]

# Stated Position
He describes his own writing on religion as coming from a traditional Roman Catholic perspective, and says he was an atheist for roughly the 1990s before that.[^feser-about][^feser-road]
