# Description
Douglas McKie was a historian of chemistry at University College London, where he held the chair in history and philosophy of science from 1957. He co-founded *Annals of Science* and edited it until his death.[^acs-mckie]

# Work
- *Antoine Lavoisier: The Father of Modern Chemistry* (1935).[^acs-mckie]
- "Wöhler's 'Synthetic' Urea and the Rejection of Vitalism: A Chemical Legend", *Nature* 153 (1944), 608–610.[^mckie-1944]
- *Antoine Lavoisier: Scientist, Economist, Social Reformer* (1952).[^acs-mckie]
