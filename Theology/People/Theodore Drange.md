# Description
Theodore M. Drange is a philosopher, emeritus at West Virginia University, who taught there from 1966 until his retirement in 2001.[^infidels-drange] He is the vault's precedent for the shape of the argument from nonbelief: take a Christian text at its face value and ask whether what it announces can be just.[^drange-1993]

# Work
- *Type Crossings* (Mouton, 1966).[^infidels-drange]
- "The Argument from Nonbelief", *Religious Studies* 29/4 (1993), 417–432.[^drange-1993]
- *Nonbelief and Evil: Two Arguments for the Nonexistence of God* (Prometheus, 1998).[^infidels-drange]

# Stated Position
In an interview he states his own conclusion, scoped to the God his argument defines: "In effect, the God of Christianity (defined to have those features) does not exist."[^infidels-interview] That is a conclusion about the specific conception his argument targets, not a general statement of atheism, and no broader one was found in print.
