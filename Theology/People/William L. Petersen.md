# Description
William L. Petersen was a New Testament and patristics scholar who taught New Testament and Christian origins at Pennsylvania State University, and before that early church history and patristics at the University of Notre Dame.[^hugoye-petersen][^gnd-petersen] He is known for his studies of Tatian's Diatessaron.[^hugoye-petersen][^petersen-1994]

# Work
- *Gospel Traditions in the Second Century: Origins, Recensions, Text, and Transmission* (University of Notre Dame Press, 1989), as editor.[^petersen-1989]
- *Tatian's Diatessaron: Its Creation, Dissemination, Significance, and History in Scholarship* (Brill, 1994).[^petersen-1994]
- "The Genesis of the Gospels" (2002).[^petersen-2002]

# Stated Position
No published statement of his own position on the truth of Christianity was found.
