# Description
Denny Burk is professor of biblical studies at Boyce College, the undergraduate school of The Southern Baptist Theological Seminary, and president of the Council on Biblical Manhood and Womanhood.[^sbts-burk] The vault cites him as evidence that the full Anselmian account of hell is still defended in print, in confessional publishing rather than academic.[^burk-2016]

# Work
- *Articular Infinitives in the Greek of the New Testament* (2006).[^sbts-burk]
- *What Is the Meaning of Sex?* (Crossway, 2013).[^sbts-burk]
- "Eternal Conscious Torment", in *Four Views on Hell*, 2nd edn, ed. Preston Sprinkle (Zondervan, 2016).[^burk-2016]

# Stated Position
On hell he holds that it "is equally everlasting and a conscious experience", and that "the unrighteous will be raised up and fit for an experience of God's wrath forever".[^sbts-burk]

[^sbts-burk]: The Southern Baptist Theological Seminary, ["Burk affirms traditional doctrine of hell in contribution to 'Four Views' book"](https://www.sbts.edu/news/burk-affirms-traditional-doctrine-of-hell-in-contribution-to-four-views-book/), 5 May 2016. His doctorate is from Southern Seminary, 2004, and he was appointed dean of Boyce College in 2008.
[^burk-2016]: Denny Burk, "Eternal Conscious Torment", in *Four Views on Hell*, 2nd edn, ed. Preston Sprinkle (Zondervan, 2016).
