# Description
Teresa Morgan is McDonald Agape Professor of New Testament and Early Christianity at Yale Divinity School, and was Professor of Graeco-Roman History at Oxford before that.[^yale-morgan] The vault cites her for the reading of *pistis* and *fides* as words for trust and trustworthiness rather than for assent to a proposition.[^morgan-2015]

# Work
- *Literate Education in the Hellenistic and Roman Worlds* (Cambridge University Press, 1998).[^yale-morgan]
- *Popular Morality in the Early Roman Empire* (Cambridge University Press, 2007).[^yale-morgan]
- *Roman Faith and Christian Faith: Pistis and Fides in the Early Roman Empire and Early Churches* (Oxford University Press, 2015).[^morgan-2015]
- *The New Testament and the Theology of Trust* (Oxford University Press, 2022).[^yale-morgan]

# Stated Position
None found in print. Her institutional page records that she is an Anglican priest, which is a fact about her orders and not a statement of her view on the questions this vault asks.[^yale-morgan]
