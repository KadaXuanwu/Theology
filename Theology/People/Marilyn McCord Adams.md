# Description
Marilyn McCord Adams was a philosopher and an Episcopal priest who held chairs at UCLA and Yale and was Regius Professor of Divinity at Oxford from 2004 to 2009. Her work on horrendous evils and on the justice of hell is what the vault's material on proportionality rests on.[^uc-adams]

# Work
- "Hell and the God of Justice", *Religious Studies* 11 (1975), 433–447.[^adams-1975]
- *Horrendous Evils and the Goodness of God* (1999).[^uc-adams]
- *Christ and Horrors: The Coherence of Christology* (2006).[^uc-adams]

# Stated Position
She said plainly in interview that she was a Christian herself, and that one of the ways God makes good on horrors is by not being aloof from them.[^adams-philosophybites]
