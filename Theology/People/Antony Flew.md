# Description
Antony Flew was a philosopher who held chairs at Keele and Reading. "Theology and Falsification" is his, and so is the argument that atheism should be the default until theism is shown, which he later gave up.[^ebsco-flew][^reading-flew]

# Work
- "Theology and Falsification" (1950).[^reading-flew]
- *God and Philosophy* (1966).[^ebsco-flew]
- *The Presumption of Atheism* (1976).[^ebsco-flew]

# Stated Position
In a 2004 interview he said the case for an Aristotelian God with power and intelligence was now much stronger than before, that he did not believe in the God of any revelatory system, and agreed that deism was the right name for his position.[^flew-habermas] The 2007 book *There Is a God* carries his name, but he later acknowledged he had not written it, so it is not usable as a statement of his views.[^ncse-flew]
