# Description
Raymond E. Brown was a Sulpician priest and New Testament scholar, and the most cited commentator on John's gospel of his century.[^brown-biography] His two volume Anchor Bible commentary on John is the reference the vault uses for that gospel, and he twice served on the Pontifical Biblical Commission.[^sulpicians-brown]

# Work
- *The Gospel According to John*, Anchor Bible 29 and 29A (1966 and 1970).[^brown-biography]
- *The Birth of the Messiah* (1977, revised 1993).[^brown-biography]
- *The Death of the Messiah*, two volumes (1994).[^brown-biography]
- *An Introduction to the New Testament* (1997).[^brown-biography]

# Stated Position
None found in print. He was ordained in 1953 and sat on the Pontifical Biblical Commission under two popes, but no published first person statement of his own about the truth of Christianity was located, and the office is not a substitute for one.
