# Description
Michael J. Murray is a philosopher of religion who held the Shadek chair at Franklin and Marshall College and later ran programmes at the John Templeton Foundation.[^avdf-murray][^vatican-murray] His paper on coercion is the standard statement of the reply that God stays hidden to protect morally significant freedom.[^murray-1993]

# Work
- "Coercion and the Hiddenness of God", *American Philosophical Quarterly* 30/1 (1993), 27–38.[^murray-1993]
- *Reason for the Hope Within*, editor (1999).[^avdf-murray]
- "Deus Absconditus", in Daniel Howard-Snyder and Paul K. Moser (eds), *Divine Hiddenness: New Essays* (2002), 62–82.[^murray-2002]
- *Nature Red in Tooth and Claw: Theism and the Problem of Animal Suffering* (2008).[^avdf-murray]
