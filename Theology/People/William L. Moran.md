# Description
William L. Moran was an Assyriologist, a Jesuit for much of his career, who taught at the Pontifical Biblical Institute in Rome and then at Harvard, where he held the Andrew W. Mellon chair in the humanities.[^gazette-moran] He edited the Amarna letters and argued that the love Deuteronomy commands is the covenant loyalty of Near Eastern treaty language.[^moran-1963][^openlibrary-amarna]

# Work
- "The Ancient Near Eastern Background of the Love of God in Deuteronomy", *Catholic Biblical Quarterly* 25 (1963), 77–87.[^moran-1963]
- *The Amarna Letters* (Johns Hopkins University Press, 1992).[^openlibrary-amarna]
- *The Most Magic Word: Essays on Babylonian and Biblical Literature*, ed. Ronald S. Hendel, Catholic Biblical Quarterly Monograph Series 35 (Catholic Biblical Association, 2002).[^cbqms-35]
