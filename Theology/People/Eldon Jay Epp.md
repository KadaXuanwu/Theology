# Description
Eldon Jay Epp is a New Testament textual critic, Harkness Professor of Biblical Literature emeritus and Dean of Humanities and Social Sciences emeritus at Case Western Reserve University.[^case-epp][^gnd-epp][^hull-2010] He took his doctorate at Harvard.[^gnd-epp]

# Work
- *The Theological Tendency of Codex Bezae Cantabrigiensis in Acts* (Cambridge University Press, 1966).[^hbz-epp-bezae]
- "The Multivalence of the Term 'Original Text' in New Testament Textual Criticism", *Harvard Theological Review* 92/3 (1999), 245–281.[^epp-1999]
- *Junia: The First Woman Apostle* (Fortress Press, 2005).[^hbz-epp-junia]
- "Are Early New Testament Manuscripts Truly Abundant?", first printed in *Israel's God and Rebecca's Children* (Baylor University Press, 2007) and reprinted in his *Perspectives on New Testament Textual Criticism*, vol. 2 (Brill, 2021), 100–145.[^hbz-epp-israel][^epp-2021]

# Stated Position
No published statement of his own position on the truth of Christianity was found.
