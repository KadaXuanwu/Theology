# Description
Michał Heller is a Polish cosmologist, philosopher and Catholic priest who works on cosmology and on the relation between science and religion. He won the Templeton Prize and founded the Copernicus Center for Interdisciplinary Studies.[^templeton-heller][^copernicus-heller]

# Work
- "Uses and Abuses of Cosmological Argumentation", *Theoria et Historia Scientiarum* 3 (1993), 36–44.[^heller-1993]
- *Creative Tension: Essays on Science and Religion* (2003).[^heller-2003]
- *Ultimate Explanations of the Universe* (2009).[^heller-2009]
- *Philosophy in Science: An Historical Introduction* (2011).[^heller-2011]

# Stated Position
At the Templeton Prize news conference in 2008: "Science gives us Knowledge, and religion gives us Meaning." Asked by scientists or philosophers how he reconciles science and religion, he said: "I invariably wonder how educated people could be so blind not to see that science does nothing else but explores God's creation."[^heller-2008] The abstract of his 1993 essay says: "It is usually the so-called God-of-gaps theology which prepares a snare for too hasty a thinker."[^heller-1993]
