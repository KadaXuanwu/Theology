# Description
Joshua C. Thurow is a professor of philosophy and chair of the Department of Philosophy and Classics at the University of Texas at San Antonio.[^utsa-thurow] He works on the atonement, a priori knowledge and what can defeat the rationality of belief in God, and wrote the *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* entry on atonement.[^thurow-sep][^casullo-thurow-2013][^thurow-2024]

# Work
- *The A Priori in Philosophy*, edited with Albert Casullo (Oxford University Press, 2013).[^casullo-thurow-2013]
- "Communal Substitutionary Atonement", *Journal of Analytic Theology* 3 (2015), 47–69.[^thurow-2015]
- With Jada Twedt Strabbing, "Entwining Thomistic and Anselmian Interpretations of the Atonement", *Faith and Philosophy* 37/4 (2020), 516–535.[^thurow-strabbing-2020]
- "He Died for Our Sins (in a Contextually-Sensitive Way)", *Journal of Analytic Theology* 9 (2021), 238–261.[^thurow-2021]
- "Atonement", *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2023).[^thurow-sep]
- *God and the Problem of Epistemic Defeaters* (Cambridge University Press, 2024).[^thurow-2024]

# Stated Position
None found in print. In the 2021 paper he writes that whether his account is true "depends upon the truth of Christianity, for which I haven't even begun to argue" (p. 258).[^thurow-2021]
