# Description
Chris Seglenieks is a New Testament scholar and adjunct lecturer at the Bible College of South Australia.[^mohr-seglenieks] The vault cites him for the reading of Johannine believing as cognitive, relational, ethical and ongoing at once, rather than as assent to a proposition.[^seglenieks-2020]

# Work
- *Johannine Belief and Graeco-Roman Devotion: Reshaping Devotion for John's Graeco-Roman Audience*, WUNT II/528 (Mohr Siebeck, 2020).[^seglenieks-2020]
- "Thomas the (Un)Faithful: Πιστός in John 20.27", *Journal for the Study of the New Testament* (2022).[^mohr-seglenieks]
- "Volition in the Johannine Writings", in *Aspects of Soteriology in John and Paul*, WUNT 529 (Mohr Siebeck, 2025).[^mohr-seglenieks]
