# Description
Christian Frevel is a scholar of the Hebrew Bible who holds the Chair of Old Testament in the Faculty of Catholic Theology at the Ruhr-Universität Bochum.[^bochum-team] He taught biblical theology at Cologne before moving to Bochum.[^frevel-cv]

# Work
- *Geschichte Israels* (2016; 2nd edn 2018).[^frevel-2018]
- *History of Ancient Israel* (2023), the revised English edition.[^sbl-2023]

# Stated Position
In the German second edition he writes that the military conquest of the land in the book of Joshua is unhistorical: "die militärische Landnahme des Josuabuches ahistorisch ist".[^frevel-2018] He also holds that rejecting the Bible wholesale as a source for Israel's history "wäre töricht", would be foolish.[^frevel-2018]
