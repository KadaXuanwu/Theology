# Description
Charles Darwin was the English naturalist whose *On the Origin of Species* set out evolution by natural selection.[^darwin-online-timeline][^barlow-1958][^vanwyhe-funeral] He studied [[William Paley]] at Cambridge (p. 59) and later wrote that Paley's argument from design "fails, now that the law of natural selection has been discovered" (p. 87).[^barlow-1958]

# Work
- *Journal of Researches* (1839), later known as *The Voyage of the Beagle*.[^darwin-online-timeline]
- *On the Origin of Species* (1859).[^darwin-online-timeline]
- *The Descent of Man, and Selection in Relation to Sex* (1871).[^darwin-online-timeline]
- *Autobiography*, written for his family from 1876. Nora Barlow's edition of 1958 restored the passages on religion that the version of 1887 had left out.[^barlow-1958]

# Stated Position
To John Fordyce, 7 May 1879: "In my most extreme fluctuations I have never been an atheist in the sense of denying the existence of a God." He went on: "I think that generally (& more and more so as I grow older) but not always, that an agnostic would be the most correct description of my state of mind."[^darwin-1879] In the *Autobiography*: "The mystery of the beginning of all things is insoluble by us; and I for one must be content to remain an Agnostic" (p. 94).[^barlow-1958]
