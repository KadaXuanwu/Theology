# Description
Neil Van Leeuwen is a philosopher of mind and action at Florida State University who collaborates with empirical researchers in anthropology, neuroscience and psychology.[^fsu-vanleeuwen] His research touches on the nature of belief and imagination, and he taught at Rutgers and Georgia State before Florida State.[^fsu-vanleeuwen][^fsu-spotlight]

# Work
- "Religious credence is not factual belief", *Cognition* 133/3 (2014), 698–715.[^vanleeuwen-2014]
- With [[Michiel van Elk]], "Seeking the Supernatural: The Interactive Religious Experience Model", *Religion, Brain & Behavior* 9/3 (2019), 221–251.[^vanleeuwen-vanelk-2019]
- *Religion as Make-Believe: A Theory of Belief, Imagination, and Group Identity* (2023).[^fsu-vanleeuwen]
