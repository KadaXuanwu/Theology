# Description
Richard Bauckham is a New Testament scholar, emeritus at St Andrews, now a senior scholar at Ridley Hall in Cambridge. *Jesus and the Eyewitnesses* is the case that the gospels rest on named eyewitness testimony rather than on anonymous tradition.[^bauckham-cv][^ridley-bauckham]

# Work
- *The Theology of the Book of Revelation* (1993).[^bauckham-cv]
- *God Crucified: Monotheism and Christology in the New Testament* (1998).[^bauckham-cv]
- *Jesus and the Eyewitnesses: The Gospels as Eyewitness Testimony* (2006, enlarged second edition 2017).[^ridley-bauckham]

# Stated Position
His own short curriculum vitae says he is an Anglican, though not ordained, and served for some years on the Church of England's Doctrine Commission.[^bauckham-cv]
