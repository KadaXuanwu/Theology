# Description
T. M. Luhrmann is a professor of anthropology at Stanford University, with a courtesy appointment in psychology.[^stanford-luhrmann] She studies how people come to experience God and other invisible beings as real, including through the practice of prayer.[^stanford-luhrmann][^npr-2012]

# Work
- *When God Talks Back* (2012), from fieldwork at the Vineyard churches.[^npr-2012]
- With Howard Nusbaum and Ronald Thisted, "'Lord, Teach Us to Pray': Prayer Practice Affects Cognitive Processing", *Journal of Cognition and Culture* 13/1–2 (2013), 159–177.[^luhrmann-2013]
- *How God Becomes Real* (Princeton University Press, 2020).[^stanford-luhrmann]

# Stated Position
"The way I think about it as an anthropologist, I don't have the authority to pronounce on whether God is real or whether God is not real," she said in 2012. "I don't feel like I have a horse in that race."[^npr-2012]
