# Description
Maarten Menken was a Dutch Catholic New Testament scholar and professor at the Catholic Theological University in Utrecht and at Tilburg University.[^gnd-menken][^koet-2016] He worked mainly on the Gospel of John, above all on how it interprets the Old Testament, and on the Old Testament text quoted in Matthew.[^koet-2016][^menken-2004]

# Work
- *Old Testament Quotations in the Fourth Gospel: Studies in Textual Form* (Kok Pharos, 1996).[^hbz-1996]
- "The Old Testament Quotation in Matthew 27,9-10: Textual Form and Context" (2002).[^menken-2002]
- *Matthew's Bible: The Old Testament Text of the Evangelist* (Leuven University Press and Peeters, 2004).[^menken-2004]

# Stated Position
No published statement of his own position on the truth of Christianity was found.
