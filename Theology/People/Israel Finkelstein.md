# Description
Israel Finkelstein is an archaeologist of ancient Israel who directed Tel Aviv University's Institute of Archaeology and co-directed the excavations at Megiddo.[^ej-finkelstein] He later became professor emeritus at Tel Aviv and head of the School of Archaeology and Maritime Cultures at the University of Haifa.[^sbl-2023]

# Work
- *The Archaeology of the Israelite Settlement* (1988).[^ej-finkelstein]
- *The Bible Unearthed*, with Neil Asher Silberman (2001).[^ej-finkelstein]
- "Patriarchs, Exodus, Conquest: Fact or Fiction?", in Israel Finkelstein and Amihai Mazar, *The Quest for the Historical Israel* (2007).[^finkelstein-mazar-2007]

# Stated Position
"The progress in archaeological and anthropological research between the 1960s and the 1980s brought about the total demise of the military conquest theory."[^finkelstein-mazar-2007] He holds that the conquest narratives "cannot be read as straightforward historical accounts", and also that "it is inconceivable that the Conquest stories were invented by the late-monarchic writers".[^finkelstein-mazar-2007]
