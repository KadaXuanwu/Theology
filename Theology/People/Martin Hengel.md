# Description
Martin Hengel was a German New Testament scholar and scholar of ancient Judaism, professor of New Testament and early Judaism at the University of Tübingen.[^enoch-hengel][^neff-2009][^dnb-hengel] He worked on the Hellenistic influence on Judaism, the Zealots, crucifixion and Judaism as the setting in which early Christianity developed.[^enoch-hengel][^hbz-crucifixion]

# Work
- *Judentum und Hellenismus* (1969), his Habilitation thesis.[^hbz-judentum][^enoch-hengel]
- *Crucifixion* (1977), the English edition of *Mors turpissima crucis*.[^hbz-crucifixion]
- *The Atonement* (1981).[^hbz-atonement]
- "The Effective History of Isaiah 53 in the Pre-Christian Period", with Daniel P. Bailey (2004).[^hengel-2004]

# Stated Position
In a 2001 essay he wrote: "The Bible's basic spiritual importance for our faith and the Christian church remains unchanged."[^hengel-2001]
