# Description
Michael W. Holmes is a New Testament textual critic and University Professor of Biblical Studies and Early Christianity emeritus at Bethel University in St Paul, Minnesota.[^bethel-holmes][^gnd-holmes] He edited the SBL edition of the Greek New Testament and an edition of the Apostolic Fathers.[^bethel-holmes]

# Work
- *The Apostolic Fathers: Greek Texts and English Translations*, 3rd ed. (Baker Academic, 2007), as editor and translator.[^bethel-holmes][^hbz-holmes-fathers]
- *The Greek New Testament: SBL Edition* (Society of Biblical Literature, 2010), as editor.[^hbz-sblgnt]
- "What Text Is Being Edited? The Editing of the New Testament" (2012).[^holmes-2012]
- *The Text of the New Testament in Contemporary Research: Essays on the Status Quaestionis*, 2nd ed. (Brill, 2013), edited with Bart D. Ehrman.[^bethel-holmes]
