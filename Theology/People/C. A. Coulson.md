# Description
Charles Alfred Coulson was a theoretical chemist, Rouse Ball Professor of Mathematics at Oxford from 1952 and then its first professor of theoretical chemistry. He was an accredited Methodist lay preacher from 1929, sat on the central committee of the World Council of Churches and chaired Oxfam.[^mactutor-coulson][^kalthoff-coulson]

# Work
- *Waves: A Mathematical Account of the Common Types of Wave Motion* (1941).[^mactutor-coulson]
- *Valence* (1952).[^mactutor-coulson]
- *Science and Christian Belief* (1955), the published form of his 1954 McNair Lectures.[^kalthoff-coulson]

# Stated Position
His own line in *Science and Christian Belief* is that either God is in the whole of nature, with no gaps, or not there at all.[^kalthoff-coulson] He did not coin the phrase god of the gaps; he put it into circulation, quoting the evangelist Henry Drummond.[^encyclopedia-gaps]
