# Description
Del Ratzsch is a philosopher of science, emeritus at Calvin University, who works on design arguments and on the limits of what natural science settles.[^calvin-ratzsch][^loc-ratzsch]

# Work
- *The Battle of Beginnings: Why Neither Side Is Winning the Creation-Evolution Debate* (1996).[^suny-ratzsch]
- *Nature, Design, and Science: The Status of Design in Natural Science* (2001).[^suny-ratzsch]
- "Teleological Arguments for God's Existence", *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, first published 2005, revised 2023 with Jeffrey Koperski.[^sep-teleological]
