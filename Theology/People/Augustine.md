# Description
Augustine taught rhetoric at Carthage, Rome and Milan, was baptised at Milan, and spent the rest of his life at Hippo Regius in Roman North Africa as priest and then bishop.[^sep-augustine][^cathen-augustine] He is also counted a philosopher, and his homilies on 1 John give the vault its account of fear as the first stage of charity.[^augustine-1john]

# Work
- *Confessions* (c. 396–400).[^sep-augustine]
- *On the Trinity*, begun in 399 and completed in 419 or as late as 426.[^sep-augustine]
- *The City of God*, begun in 412 and completed in 426.[^sep-augustine]
- *Homilies on the First Epistle of John*, placed by Philip Schaff's preface at about 416 or later, alongside the tractates on John's Gospel.[^augustine-1john][^schaff-1888]

# Stated Position
On fear and love, in Homily 9: "Fear, so to say, prepares a place for charity. But when once charity has begun to inhabit, the fear which prepared the place for it is cast out."[^augustine-1john]

[^cathen-augustine]: Eugène Portalié, ["Life of St. Augustine of Hippo"](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/02084a.htm), *Catholic Encyclopedia*, vol. 2 (Robert Appleton Company, 1907). It records the teaching posts at Carthage, Rome and Milan, the baptism at Easter 387, ordination at Hippo in 391 and the episcopate from 395 or 396.
