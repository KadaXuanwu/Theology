# Description
Francis A. Sullivan was an American Jesuit who taught ecclesiology at the Pontifical Gregorian University in Rome for thirty-six years and then at Boston College.[^jesuits-sullivan] The vault cites him as the standard history of what the Catholic church has said about the salvation of people outside it.[^sullivan-1992]

# Work
- *Magisterium: Teaching Authority in the Catholic Church* (1983).[^jesuits-sullivan]
- *Salvation Outside the Church? Tracing the History of the Catholic Response* (Paulist Press, 1992).[^sullivan-1992]
- *Creative Fidelity: Weighing and Interpreting Documents of the Magisterium* (1996).[^jesuits-sullivan]

None found in print.
