# Description
Frank Keil is a psychologist at Yale who works on how concepts develop and on how much of an explanation people believe they hold. He taught at Cornell for over twenty years before moving to Yale in 1998.[^yale-keil][^yale-news-keil]

# Work
- *Concepts, Kinds, and Cognitive Development* (1989).[^yale-keil]
- "The misunderstood limits of folk science: an illusion of explanatory depth", with Leonid Rozenblit, *Cognitive Science* 26/5 (2002), 521–562.[^rozenblit-keil]
- *Wonder: Childhood and the Lifelong Love of Science* (2022).[^yale-keil]
