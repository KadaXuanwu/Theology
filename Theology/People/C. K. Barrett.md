# Description
C. K. Barrett was a British New Testament scholar and Methodist minister who taught at Durham University from 1945, as professor of divinity from 1958 until he retired in 1982.[^dunn-2013] His commentary on John was first published in 1955 and revised in 1978.[^guardian-barrett]

# Work
- *The Gospel According to St John* (SPCK, 1955; 2nd edn 1978).[^dunn-2013]
- *A Commentary on the Epistle to the Romans* (1957).[^dunn-2013]
- *The Gospel of John and Judaism* (1975).[^dunn-2013]
- *A Critical and Exegetical Commentary on the Acts of the Apostles*, International Critical Commentary, 2 vols (1994 and 1998).[^dunn-2013]

# Stated Position
In the preface to his commentary on 2 Corinthians he wrote: "Like most people, I sometimes wonder whether Christianity is true; but I think I never doubt that, if it is true, it is truest in the form it took with Paul".[^dunn-2013]
