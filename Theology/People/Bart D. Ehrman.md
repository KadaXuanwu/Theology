# Description
Bart D. Ehrman is a New Testament scholar and James A. Gray Distinguished Professor at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.[^ehrman-about][^gnd-ehrman] He works on the text of the New Testament and the history of early Christianity.[^ehrman-about]

# Work
- *The Orthodox Corruption of Scripture: The Effect of Early Christological Controversies on the Text of the New Testament* (Oxford University Press, 1993).[^hbz-ehrman-corruption]
- *The Text of the New Testament: Its Transmission, Corruption, and Restoration*, 4th ed. (Oxford University Press, 2005), with Bruce M. Metzger.[^hbz-ehrman-text]
- *Misquoting Jesus: The Story Behind Who Changed the Bible and Why* (HarperSanFrancisco, 2005).[^hbz-ehrman-misquoting]
- *The New Testament: A Historical Introduction to the Early Christian Writings*, 6th ed. (Oxford University Press, 2016).[^hbz-ehrman-introduction]

# Stated Position
In *God's Problem* he writes that he "came to realize that I could no longer believe in the God of my tradition, and acknowledged that I was an agnostic".[^ehrman-2008]
