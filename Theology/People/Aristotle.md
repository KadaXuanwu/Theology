# Description
Aristotle studied under Plato at the Academy, tutored Alexander, and founded the Lyceum in Athens. His explanations of weather and the sky give no god a causal role, which is why the *Meteorologica* turns up in arguments about what counts as a gap.[^sep-aristotle]

# Work
- *Metaphysics*.[^sep-aristotle]
- *Physics*.[^sep-aristotle]
- *Meteorologica*.[^sep-aristotle]
- *Nicomachean Ethics*.[^sep-aristotle]

# Stated Position
None, and the question does not arise: he died about three centuries before Christianity existed.


[^sep-aristotle]: ["Aristotle"](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/aristotle/), *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*. The chronology of the surviving treatises is unsettled, so no years are given for individual works.
