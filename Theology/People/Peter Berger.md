# Description
Peter L. Berger was an Austrian-born American sociologist of religion who spent his later career at Boston University, where he founded and directed its Institute on Culture, Religion and World Affairs.[^bu-berger] The vault cites him for plausibility structures, and for his later verdict that secularisation theory, to which his own early work contributed, is "essentially mistaken".[^bullivant-2011][^berger-1999]

# Work
- *The Social Construction of Reality*, with Thomas Luckmann (1966).[^bu-berger][^pfadenhauer-2017]
- *The Sacred Canopy: Elements of a Sociological Theory of Religion* (1967).[^pfadenhauer-2017]
- *A Rumor of Angels: Modern Society and the Rediscovery of the Supernatural* (1969).[^pfadenhauer-2017]
- *The Desecularization of the World: Resurgent Religion and World Politics*, edited (1999).[^berger-1999]

# Stated Position
"My religious views have been pretty much the same throughout my career. I was and I am a moderate Lutheran, not a very good Lutheran, but a Lutheran. In some ways I'm still a Lutheran."[^eppc-berger]
