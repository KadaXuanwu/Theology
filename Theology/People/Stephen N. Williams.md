# Description
Stephen N. Williams is a theologian who was Professor of Systematic Theology at Union Theological College, Belfast, from 1994 to 2017.[^faraday-williams] He wrote the "Theological Horizons of Joshua" part of a commentary on Joshua with J. Gordon McConville.[^butler-2010]

# Work
- *Joshua*, with J. Gordon McConville, The Two Horizons Old Testament Commentary (Eerdmans, 2010).[^butler-2010]
- "Could God Have Commanded the Slaughter of the Canaanites?", *Tyndale Bulletin* 63/2 (2012), 161–178.[^williams-2012]
- *The Election of Grace: A Riddle without a Resolution?* (Eerdmans, 2015).[^faraday-williams]

# Stated Position
In his 2012 article he holds that the slaughter should not be conceived as a possible divine command unless it was "sorrowfully commanded", accommodated to human violence. He leaves open whether God in fact commanded it.[^williams-2012]
