**What the node says:** Bloch is listed under Source. Nothing in the note rests on him.
**What is open:** this is the standard work on the practice and none of it was read. The note was built from a single 2025 article. **What would settle it:** the 1973 translation. Whether anything in the note is wrong or too narrow by Bloch's account, and whether the French rite belongs in it.

### V2 — Koch's dates from catalogue records
**Status:** open
**Node:** `Evidence/The Royal Touch`
**Source:** Robert Koch, announcement to the Berlin Physiological Society, 24 March 1882, published in *Berliner klinische Wochenschrift* that year; "Die Aetiologie der Tuberkulose", *Mittheilungen aus dem Kaiserlichen Gesundheitsamte* 2 (1884), 1–88
**What the node says:** the bacterial cause of tuberculosis was identified in 1882, with the 1884 paper following.
**What is open:** the date, the venue and the 1884 pagination all come from catalogue and repository records, not from the papers. The claim is uncontroversial and appears the same way everywhere, so this is a low risk item, but it was not read at source.
**What would settle it:** the Robert Koch Institute's digitised copy, or any history of medicine that carries the date from the primary.
