Crux: The argument stands or falls on two premises the wager fragment does not state, and which of them decides it is open. The first is that the act which makes the infinite gain likelier is preferred; Oppy and Herzberg 2011 grant it for anyone whose chance of God is not infinitesimal, and Hájek's dilemma asks whether a repaired value still makes salvation the best good. The second is that no rival with the same promise, such as Islam, gets as high a chance as the Christian God; it turns on whether a decision may drop a rival held at some chance but with no pull, as Jordan's restriction to live hypotheses requires, and failing that on the evidence for each revelation.
